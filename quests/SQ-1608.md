--- note 2026-09-26T19:51:12Z ---

Shogun's boot menu ('START the game' with its selection bar, below 'You may choose to:') is chrome the game prints over an ordinary story window. hybrid_story_slot_grid returns None there, and the terminal renderer's overlay (render/screen.rs ~1397-1605) packs those runs into the viewport's cells. A host drawing Hybrid itself can only place them at their native pixel position, which overlaps 'You may choose to:' at phone width. Wanted: publish that overlay in the layout (each run's col, row and resolved span in the viewport's cells), for example V6HybridChromeLayout::story_overlay.
