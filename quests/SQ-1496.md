FINDINGS (investigation lane, 2026-09-16) — SOLVED for all three titles, and the answer is NOT a hand table: the (usage, index) → record association is ON SIDE A, in a table the game program reads. §12.10 is right that the DATABASE has none; the program area of the same disk does.

## 1. The table (measured on all three bitmap titles, identical layout)

Side A file offset **0x9593**, preceded at 0x9590 by three bytes of the AI adventure number (`05 05 05` Count, `04 04 04` Voodoo, `0D 0D 0D` Claymorgue — the same marker side B carries at 0x290). **190 two-byte entries**:
- entries [0..99]  = ROOM-usage picture index i (§8.6: 0 darkness, 1..N rooms, 80+ close-ups, 99 title);
- entries [100..189] = OBJECT picture index (i−100), one slot per item index;
- an all-zero entry = no picture. Slot 98 is zero on all three (see §3).
Extent is 190, not 200: on Voodoo Castle the bytes from 0x970F on are leftover picture run-length triples, not entries; Count/Claymorgue are zero there.

Entry `[A, S]` decodes as:
```
sector      = ((A & 3) << 8) | S          // 1-based 128-byte Atari sector; file offset = 16 + (sector-1)*128
byte        = (A >> 3) * 7                 // byte within the sector (0..126)
inventory   = A & 4                        // object entries only: set = drawn on the INVENTORY screen (§8.6 "B…I"), clear = drawn in the ROOM ("B…R")
file_offset = 16 + (sector-1)*128 + byte   // = the record's header byte 0 (its size word), exactly where scan_picture_side finds it
```
The ×7 is real, not a coincidence: EVERY one of the 241 records on the three sides starts at a multiple of seven bytes into its sector (byte-in-sector ∈ {0,7,14,…,126}) — that is what the "nought to six bytes of filler" between records are for. Spliced coordinates for `record_at`/`decode_record`: subtract VTOC_LEN (128) for file offsets ≥ 0xB410.

Verification: all 189 non-zero entries across the three titles resolve to the header offset of a record `scan_picture_side` located — the only two exceptions are The Count's two damaged records (SQ-1498), which the table names at EXACTLY the offsets the scan skips (0x7CBA for room 6, 0xF72C for room 16). Room entries never carry the inventory bit. Pinned by the new specimen test `side_a_carries_the_picture_table_and_every_entry_names_a_located_record` (crates/scott/tests/saga_atari_specimens.rs).

## 2. Cross-checks that the table means what it says
- Rooms: every room-usage entry i ≤ N points at the record whose picture matches the Apple II scrambled release's record i (SQ-1490/1499 settled those). Automated luminance+edge correlation, downsampled 4×4: Count all ≥0.82 (next-best ≤0.60), Claymorgue all ≥0.65 (mostly ≥0.9); Voodoo scores are low (0.3–0.6) because the Atari palette is inverted relative to the Apple one, but every pair was checked by eye and matches. Text anchors: darkness card "IT'S TOO DARK TO SEE!" at [0] on all three; death cards at the last room ([22] LOT OF TROUBLE graveyard, [25] Count Cristo face, [32] skull in wizard hat); Count room 1 = brass bed; Claymorgue room 17 = an 80-byte flat blue fill (the Apple's is a flat blue field too).
- Title [99]: Count "WELCOME TO THE COUNT" card; Voodoo "ai Adventure International / VooDoo CASTLE"; Claymorgue "Adventure International Proudly Presents S.A.G.A. 13".
- Close-ups: Count [80] = parcel "TO DRACULA FROM YORGA" (= Apple 80), [81] = crowd at the gate (= Apple 81). Voodoo has only FOUR close-ups and numbers them 81–84: [81] = broken sword (Apple 87), [82] = chemical flasks (Apple 85), [83] = open coffin (Apple 88), [84] = seated idol (Apple 84); no [80], no doll/book/ring/ju-ju/knife close-ups on the Atari. Claymorgue has none.
- Objects: a large sample identified from the sprite itself against the item text — e.g. Count [123] three tablets = "3 no-doz tablets", [124] two = "2 nodoz tablets", [108] one = "1 nodoz tablet", [158] = portrait of Dracula, [161] = daisies, [137] = pocket watch, [167] = skeleton with stake; Voodoo [134]/[137] = open/closed safe, [143]/[109] = dusty/glowing idol (same slot position, two states), [123] = the Spirit Medium; Claymorgue spell scrolls are lettered boxes — [124] FI, [133] ME, [134] SE, [136] LI, [140] PE, [141] BL, [145] YOHO, [157] FIFI, [161] WQ, [164] UN, [170] DD, [173] LY — all landing on the matching "… spell" item. Inventory-flagged entries are exactly the carriable items (auto_noun) with grid-like positions; unflagged ones are scenery placed at their in-room positions (composites of r041 over room 8, r034 over room 9, r042 over room 18, r039 over room 21, Voodoo r078 over room 14 all sit where the room picture expects them).

## 3. The inventory backdrop is reached by a fixed pointer, not slot 98
Slot [98] is zero on all three. Instead side A **0x984F** holds one more `[A, S]` entry, on all three titles, pointing at the one record the table does not name: a tiny near-full-canvas card of three flat colour bars (dark red / green / blue):
- Count  r043 @0x03000, 43 bytes, 240x126 at (0,0), colours [30,87,DC,B0]
- Voodoo r077 @0x16590, 43 bytes, 264x126 at (0,0), colours [30,87,DC,B0]
- Clay   r055 @0x05580, 61 bytes, 232x128 at (16,0), colours [32,87,E8,C6]
That matches §12.11's inventory routine ("clears the graphics window, draws this index as a room picture, then draws the inventory-object picture of every carried item"; "on the Atari 8-bit this image comes from the companion disk") — the flagged sprites are drawn over these bars at their own positions. These three cards are the ONLY records on the three sides not referenced by the 0x9593 table. Note the fourth colour byte is non-zero only here (B0/C6) — §8.3 says the fourth byte is unused; on this card it may be the background register. Unverified; do not act on it.

## 5. Geometry facts the renderer will meet
Full-canvas records come in several header shapes, not one: Count 280x158 [3,0,38,158] and 296x126 [2,0,39,126]; Voodoo 296x126, 288x158 [2,0,38,158] and 320x160 [0,0,43,160]; Claymorgue 288x160 and 304x128. `paint_strips` already clips; the 126/128-row ones simply leave the bottom of the canvas black. Object sprites carry their absolute placement (§8.6) and paint only their box.

## 6. Negative results / not done
- No table keyed by record offset or sector exists on side B (SQ-1483 was right about side B); exhaustive searches for LE16/BE16 offsets, sector numbers, (sector,byte) triples and 24-bit offsets on both sides found only this one table on side A.
- The trigger conditions for the item-less object indices (Count 80/82/83/84, Voodoo 70) were not read out of the program.
- The C64 archive holds only the Hulk, so the cross-platform anchor was the Apple II scrambled release, not C64.
- Nothing in crates/app or production saga_atari.rs was touched; only test-only pins were added (see commit).

## 7. Implementation sketch for the follow-up lane
`scott::saga_atari`: add a reader `picture_table(side_a: &[u8]) -> Vec<TableEntry { usage: Room|ObjectInRoom|ObjectInInventory, index: u8, file_offset: usize }>` walking 190 entries at 0x9593 (optionally locate it by the `[adv,adv,adv]` marker at 0x9590 rather than trusting the constant), plus `inventory_backdrop(side_a)` from 0x984F; validate each entry with `record_at(&spliced, spliced_of(file_offset), scheme)` so garbage after the table (Voodoo) is refused rather than drawn. Host side: mounting must pair side A with its side B (`[side B]` / `_ side B` filename twins); `SagaUs::room_picture` identity + this table give the record; INVENTORY_PICTURE 98 is served by the 0x984F card; the two damaged Count records are SQ-1498. Method note for the clean-room log: side A's program bytes were read as a specimen (as `apple_look_table` did for M2); no interpreter source was consulted. Scratch tooling lived in the session scratchpad and is not committed.
