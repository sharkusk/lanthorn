Refinement (user, 2026-09-03), supersedes the previous note's "outgoing slots" wording: a one-way connector must not arrive on ANY of the destination's eight compass slots (the four mid-side arrowhead cells and the four corner/diagonal departure cells), occupied or not — an arrowhead landing on the north slot reads as "north leads back here", a return path that does not exist.
