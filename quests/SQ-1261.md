Map affordance (user design 2026-09-02): a `?` direction draws a one-cell outgoing stub from the room box in that direction, terminated by a superscript count of the distinct destination rooms recorded for it (`╰───²───╯` on a cardinal side; at the corner for a diagonal), and nothing beyond it — no connector. Bare `?` stub (no count) when no distinct destination has been recorded, including the rename-loop case where the "destination" is the same room under other names. The count is the same set the room panel names and the matrix cell shows (`?³`); all cleared when the direction upgrades to a real edge. The stub glyph/count is a new box marker: ColorScheme field + style.rs selector + render apply, documented via the generated style reference.
