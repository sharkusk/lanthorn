--- note 2026-09-09T05:35:25Z ---

Raised out of SQ-1414 (2026-09-09). lanthorn is BSD-3-Clause; ScottFree/Gargoyle's terps/scott, Frotz and ScottKit are GPL. SQ-1414 found `crates/scott/src/loader.rs`'s Dialect signatures documented with `detectgame.c:44-46`-style citations and fixed them (22620db7), but that is unlikely to be the only site — CLAUDE.md and the crate docs actively instruct lanes to check behaviour against these interpreters, and `crates/scott/src/lib.rs` and `vm.rs` are full of "ScottCurses.c:NNNN" citations that name GPL source as the authority for this crate's behaviour.

Two separable questions, and they have different answers:

1. BEHAVIOUR checked against a reference (run the binary, compare output) is fine and is what MEMORY.md's "check behaviour against a reference rendition" asks for. Citing a GPL FILE AND LINE NUMBER as the source of a constant or an algorithm is a different thing, and is what needs auditing. `crates/scott/vm.rs` alone has dozens of `ScottCurses.c:NNN` citations; whether those describe (a) behaviour someone observed and then located in the source, or (b) code that was transcribed, cannot be told apart from the comment, which is precisely the problem.

2. What the POLICY should be, written down where a lane will see it (CLAUDE.md): which reference implementations may be read at all, that a LICENSE must be checked per path before reading (Gargoyle aggregates GPL, MIT, BSD and Artistic-licensed terps under one repo, so "Gargoyle is GPL" and "Gargoyle is MIT" are both wrong as blanket statements), and what a doc comment may cite.

Suggested scope: grep the tree for citations of third-party source files (`ScottCurses.c`, `detectgame.c`, `*.c:` line refs, "Gargoyle", "Spatterlight", "Frotz", "ScottKit", "ScottFree", "Bocfel"), classify each as observed-behaviour vs transcribed, and decide per site. Then write the policy.

--- note 2026-09-09T05:35:40Z ---

Scoping measurement (2026-09-09, from the SQ-1414 lane): 116 line-level citations of third-party C source in `crates/scott` alone — options.rs 42, vm.rs 43, scottfree_save.rs 3, loader.rs 3 (now format-attributed), lib.rs 1, plus the rest. Beyond scott, 20+ files across `crates/app/tests/suites/`, `crates/app/src/` and `crates/scott-cli/` mention Gargoyle / Spatterlight / ScottFree / Frotz / Bocfel by name. So this is a tree-wide audit, not a one-file fix, and the volume is the argument for writing the policy FIRST and then sweeping — otherwise each lane re-decides.

Worth noting which way the risk runs: `options.rs`'s and `vm.rs`'s citations are mostly of the form "this reply string / this comparison is what ScottFree does at line N", i.e. observable behaviour with a source pointer attached for the reader's benefit. That is the benign end. The dangerous end is a citation standing in for a CONSTANT or a TABLE nobody measured — which is exactly what loader.rs's Dialect signatures were, and what `scottgameinfo.c`'s 66-entry per-game offset table would have been had SQ-1414 gone ahead as briefed.
