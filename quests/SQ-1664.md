  Found while fixing SQ-1662: when an Inform object has no printed name, typeable_name's fallback (vocab.rs ~line 568, obj.words.first()) picks the FIRST stored dictionary word rather than the most descriptive one. King of Shreds and Patches' "John Croft's letter" has words ["john","croft's","handwritt","note","letter"] and the fallback picks "john" instead of "letter" — already affects inventory_click_words (command band) today, and SQ-1662 (inventory_items_with_keys) now exposes it in the live Carrying: panel too (crates/app/tests/suites/glulx_inventory.rs::king_of_shreds_panels_are_filled_at_the_first_prompt, test assertion updated to match rather than fixed). Separately, 5 distinct penny objects in the same game share an identical words list (["penny","pennies","coin","silver","coins"]) so they are genuinely indistinguishable to the parser and collapse to "penny" x5 — that part is correct, not a bug.
