--- note 2026-08-29T05:18:03Z ---

## The report

Arthur. The torque's own description names **a crystal embedded in the knob**.
Both are examinable — `examine knob` opens the hint menu — and **neither the
reveal nor the command band knows either word**.

The description tells the player the crystal is there, and the two surfaces whose
job is to say what is real then deny it. That is worse than saying nothing.

## The cause

`vocab::objects_in_scope` (`vocab.rs:933`) is **one level deep**:

```rust
if let Some(r) = room  { out.extend(intro.room_objects_excluding(r, player)); }
if let Some(p) = player { out.extend(intro.contents(p)); }
```

The room's direct contents, plus the player's direct contents. Nothing descends.
The knob is *part of* the torque and the crystal is *in* the knob — two levels
below anything the walk looks at.

**This was known and deliberately left.** SQ-1042's lane wrote: *"Zork I's open
glass bottle does not list its water through `visible_room_objects`; the walk
under-reports rather than over-reports, which is the safe direction and not my
rule to relax."* Safe was right for a first pass. The torque is the case that
shows under-reporting is not harmless.

## What descending must NOT break

The band already has a rule worth preserving: *here* **never lists the contents
of a closed container** — Zork I's lunch and garlic stay hidden inside the brown
sack until it is opened, and the leaflet stays in the mailbox. Descending must
respect that, or the reveal becomes a spoiler engine that lights everything the
player has not opened yet.

So the walk needs the story's own notion of what is transparent: an **open**
container, a **supporter**, and a **part**. The Z-machine gives attributes no
fixed meaning, so which attribute means "open" is recovered per story — the band
already does this for its `here` column (`command_band.rs`), and that recovery is
the thing to reuse rather than re-derive.

Depth: unbounded is wrong (a container in a container in a container is a
performance and noise question); one more level may be enough for the reported
case. **Measure against real stories before choosing**, and say what you chose.

## The reveal has a SECOND, unrelated cause on this story

`reveal.rs`'s own docs: *"Version 6 raster draws its text as bitmap glyphs on a
canvas… `visible_text` reads the CELL wrap cache, so on raster nothing lights and
`arm` says so rather than appearing to work."*

**Arthur in extended mode is a raster path**, so the reveal was never going to
light anything there, crystal or not. Fixing scope alone will fix the **band** on
Arthur and leave the reveal dark.

That is honest behaviour rather than a bug, and SQ-1107 scoped raster out
deliberately — but it is now a second thing the user has hit, and it means the
reveal is unavailable on exactly the graphical stories where a description is
densest. Worth its own quest if it is wanted; do not smuggle it into this one.

## Verify against the parser

`examine crystal` and `examine knob` must both be accepted by the real game on
the real story before and after — the parser is the oracle, as SQ-1118 and
SQ-1042 both established. And check the closed-container rule still holds on Zork
I's brown sack in the same run.

--- note 2026-08-29T15:09:37Z ---

--- lane close-out ---

Closed on the defect the "cause" section above names — **the carried half of
scope was one level deep while the room half had nested to three since
SQ-0678** — measured, fixed and falsified. The **reported Arthur symptom is
still live** and is a different defect: split out as [[SQ-1134]].

## What was fixed

`WorldModel::visible_contents` (the room walk minus the shared-scenery pass,
which is a room's property and nothing a rucksack has) →
`Introspect::visible_contents` → `vocab::scope_split`, the one spelling of
"here and carried" the reveal, Tab completion and the band's columns now read.

Specimen, parser-verified: Mini-Zork r34/s871124 (tracked, so it runs on CI)
and Zork I r88/s840726, Kitchen, six turns in — `n`, `e`, `open window`,
`enter window`, `take sack`, `open sack`. The sack in your hands listed nothing
below itself; `i` prints "The brown sack contains: A lunch / A clove of
garlic" and `examine lunch` answers.

## Measured depth: ONE, capped at MAX_NEST_DEPTH

- Driven need is 1: both releases put the lunch and the garlic exactly one
  level under the opened sack.
- An unbounded, openness-respecting walk of every Z-story in `stories/`
  (62 booted, 12 taps in) reaches depth 0 under the player in **all** of them —
  nobody carries an opened container on turn 0 — and the openness attribute is
  confidently identified in only **4 of the 62**, so where it is not, nothing
  nests at any depth.
- The cap taken is the room walk's own `MAX_NEST_DEPTH`, shared rather than
  chosen again: a second cap here would be exactly the hand-maintained
  invariant that produced this bug.

## What was NOT fixed, and why it is not depth

Arthur r74/s890714, churchyard, 12 taps: `#112 "sliver of crystal"
[crystal/sliver/knob/knobs]` is ONE level under `#224 "torque"` — well inside
the room walk's existing reach. It is excluded by the OPENNESS test.
Arthur's true open bit is attribute 42; `infer_open_attr` rejects it at filter
2 (Jaccard 0.65 against a 0.6 bar) and accepts attribute 44, the animate bit.
Loosening the bar lets 42 through and leaves 44 standing, so filter 5 answers
`None` and Arthur nests nothing at all — strictly worse. Full measurements,
and the two candidate signals that would fix it, are in [[SQ-1134]].

The second cause this quest already noted — the reveal is dark on Arthur
because extended mode is a raster path — is untouched and still true.
