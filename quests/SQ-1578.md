--- note 2026-09-25T19:50:44Z ---

Measured at rev d7fd62b7 with the lib's own `Rasterise` composite (`compose_v6_frame` + `bottom_anchor_menu`, 1000×848 pane). A RecordOnly host reproduces it pixel for pixel, so this is in the lib.

1. **Amiga r30 (`Journey - The Quest Begins.adf`, line drawing).** The story bottom is row 288, the extension adds 128 rows, and the band starts at 416.
   | column | rows 280–287 | rows 288–415 (extension) |
   |---|---|---|
   | left border | `ff` at x=3 only (1 px) | `ff` across x=0..7 |
   | divider | `ff` at x=259 only | `ff` across x=256..263 |
   | right border | `ff` at x=635 only | `ff` across x=632..639 |

   **Cause:** `crates/app/src/render/screen.rs:7721` does `Some(bg.unwrap_or(fg))`. For a divider drawn as a plain `│` glyph with no background, the fill takes the glyph's ink, and ~l.7735–7738 then paint it over the whole 8-px cell (`bx0..bx1`). The TUI's own `flank_border_extension` (screen.rs:6876, `seek_ink` ~6919) measures the actual stroke columns instead.
2. **PC r83 (`journey-r83-s890706.z6`, reverse fill).** The story bottom is row 304 and the band starts at 432. The right bar at x=632..639 is `dc` for rows 16–287, `00` for rows 288–303, then `dc` again from 304 down. The fill starts at `story_bottom` (`for y in story_bottom..avail_h`, ~l.7735), so the empty text row between where the bar ends (288) and the story's foot (304) stays as a notch in the middle of the extended bar.

**Change:** extend flank borders the way `flank_border_extension` does: carry each border's real stroke columns and colours down, and start from where the border actually ends, not from `story_bottom`. Share that rule rather than keeping a second one.

**Acceptance:**
- Both releases on a tall pane with the Menu plan: every border and divider column keeps the same pixels in the extension as in the 8 rows above it.
- The PC right bar is continuous from row 16 to the band.
- Frame/Extend titles (Zork Zero, Shogun, Arthur) are unchanged.
