--- note 2026-08-31T01:38:10Z ---

Measured, and the leak is not there. Discarding with the evidence.

METHOD. A throwaway `crates/app/examples/boot_seed_probe.rs` (built, run, deleted)
routed every file in `stories/` through `hints::load_mounted_story_from` — the same
mount `startup.rs` uses, so disk images, zips and Blorbs are covered, and
`hints::mounted_stories` enumerated every named entry on the multi-story images —
then read `Machine::pending_sounds`, `Machine::diagnostics` and
`Machine::pending_pictures` STRAIGHT AFTER the constructor, i.e. after the game's
own boot and before any host turn. 277 Z-machine boots and 26 Glulx boots
(37 Scott skipped — `TurnResult.sounds`/`diagnostics`/`pictures` are all empty
there by construction). Glulx cannot be read directly (`GlulxSession::machine` is
`pub(crate)`), so its half booted, drained the banner the way `startup.rs` does,
and took ONE turn through the public `Engine` trait, inspecting that turn's
`glulx_sound_ops` / `diagnostics` / `sounds`.

RESULT, per fact:

* PICTURES — the claim is simply wrong: a boot drain already exists, it is just not
  inside `seed_turn`. `GameSession::flush_boot_pictures` (session.rs:1136) is called
  from startup.rs:1419, reset.rs:209/541 and turn.rs:1367, unconditionally in the
  ZCode arm and BEFORE the `seed_turn` at startup.rs:1864. It has to be, because a
  v6 game's opening art must be on the canvas for the first `screen()`, which is
  drawn before the player types anything. Measured: 44 v6 boots leave pending
  pictures (advent.z6 9, arthur-r74 9, scopa.z6 220, shogun-r322 5, zork0-r393 20,
  mysterious01-11 10 each, plus every disk press — Arthur .adf/.2mg/.po, Journey
  .adf, Shogun .adf/.po/.toast/.dsk, Zork Zero .adf/.po/.dsk/.ima/.image,
  InfocomMasterpieces.img), and every one is drained before the seed.
* SOUNDS — nothing drains `machine.pending_sounds` between the constructor and turn
  1, but NO story in the corpus leaves one there. Zero across all 303 boots. That
  includes both Infocom sound titles (lurkinghorror-r219-s870912.z3 and
  sherlock-r26-s880127.z5, booted with `sound_available: true`, and Sherlock's
  .adf/.img presses) and the only three Glulx games in `stories/` with `Snd `
  resources at all (Kerkerkruip.gblorb, Kerkerkruip.b10.gblorb, sensory.blorb).
* DIAGNOSTICS — same shape: no drain, and no story raises one at boot. Zero across
  all 303 boots.

THE ROUTE IS OPEN, THOUGH, AND WAS FORCED. Setting Flags 2 bit 0 in `advent.z8`'s
header before boot makes `Machine::check_transcript_bit` (exec.rs:1025) raise its
`output_stream 2` warning at the BOOT's own input request. Observed:
`machine.diagnostics` non-empty after the constructor; `seed_turn().diagnostics ==
[]`; `submit("look").diagnostics == [that warning]`. So the mechanism the quest
guessed at is real; it just has no reproducer.

WHY NOT FIX IT ANYWAY. Draining sounds/diagnostics into `seed_turn` would not
deliver them at the boot, it would DISCARD them: `startup.rs` feeds `seed_result`
to `apply_turn` (the mapper) and nothing on that path plays a sound or pushes a
Warning line. A real fix is "surface a boot sound and a boot warning AT the boot",
which is a behaviour change with no story to validate it against and nothing to
falsify it with — exactly the shape this project has been bitten by. It waits for a
story that needs one.

Recorded where the next reader meets it: `Engine::seed_turn`'s doc in
crates/app/src/engine.rs now states the audit, the corpus numbers, the
`flush_boot_pictures` pointer and the forced-diagnostic recipe, so this does not get
re-filed from the same reasoning. Doc-only change; `cargo check --all-targets`
clean, `boot_screen_clear` 4/4 pass, `cargo clippy -p app --lib` clean.
