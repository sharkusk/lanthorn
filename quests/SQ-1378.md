Cause (read, not bisected): main.rs ~3804, the SQ-0566 v6 mouse delivery (4a854a72, 2026-07-29). A left-Down inside the drawn v6 image is handed to the VM IMMEDIATELY as ZSCII 254 whenever the story wants the mouse and lists a click among its line terminators — Zork Zero, Shogun and Arthur all do (254 or the 255 wildcard); Journey does not, text games have no v6 image. `map_click` covers the story text too, so every press in the pane ends the line read as a click and `StartSelection` never runs. Not the shm work, not SQ-1340; broken since July. Fix design: defer the game click the way the map defers its own (SQ-1325 BeginMapDrag/EndDragPan): press-release without motion delivers 254 exactly as today; press-drag becomes a text selection anchored at the Down cell and delivers nothing.
