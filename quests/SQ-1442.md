  Found while implementing SQ-1422 (Versions 1 and 2). §3.7: "any multi-Z-character constructions should be left incomplete (rather than omitted) if there's no room to finish them" — encode_word_impl instead breaks out entirely when a shift plus body would overrun the 6/9 Z-char budget, so the trailing Z-chars become padding 5s rather than a truncated escape. Bocfel's dict.cpp writes into a 12-slot array and truncates, i.e. leaves them incomplete. Pre-existing for every version, not introduced by SQ-1422; affects only words whose final character needs the 10-bit escape or a shift at the budget boundary, so it can silently miss a dictionary word.
