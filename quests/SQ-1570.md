Current code (rev 5844eb4d):
- The pacing driver is `loop_tick::poll_picture_pacing` / `settle_picture_pacing` (crates/app/src/loop_tick.rs:428-474), bin-only.
- `picture_pace_next` isn't folded into `host::clock::next_deadline` (crates/app/src/host/clock.rs:150), so a host's sleep/wait deadline misses paced pictures unless it adds its own.
- Result: every host re-writes ~15 lines of the tick and its own deadline (Zork Zero's compass animation is paced after each move — 4 frames 33–51 ms apart — and Arthur's intro plates).

Change: move them into the lib as `host::clock::poll_picture_pacing(state, session) -> bool` (true = something revealed, repaint) and `settle_picture_pacing(...)`, and have `next_deadline` include `picture_pace_next`. The TUI's loop_tick calls the lib functions.

Acceptance:
- Test: Zork Zero r393, after a move, `next_deadline` reports the next paced frame; polling at each deadline reveals the 4 compass frames in order; settle reveals any remaining at once.
- TUI behaviour unchanged (existing pacing tests pass).
