--- note 2026-09-27T19:41:26Z ---

crates/app/src/history.rs: record_turn (~36) stores map_snapshot only when map_changed. When recording is switched on mid-game (the map already has rooms), or after cap_history (~64) evicts the record that held the last snapshot, map_at_turn (~73) returns None for every turn until the map next changes; a viewer then has no map for those turns (the terminal shows an empty map). Seen with Lost Pig: switch recording on after exploring, then take turns that find no new room.
Ask: store a baseline snapshot with the first record written into an empty history, and make sure the oldest retained record always carries one after eviction (e.g. cap_history copies the evicted baseline forward).
