## Shipped as `confirm` — the measurement supports it, one sample wants an eye

`MIN_LEN` moved from the top of `StoryVocabulary::offer` into
`by_near_miss`, where its reasoning (edit distance) actually lives. The
exact sources — WordNet's exception list via `stems`, the synonym table
via `by_meaning` — now reach `offer`'s dictionary intersection at any
length. `by_near_miss` gates on BOTH sides now: the dictionary word as
before, and the typed word, which is the half that used to sit at the top.

### The false-positive census

`/usr/share/dict/words`, 1142 three-letter words, against ten real
stories (Zork I r88, Planetfall r37, Enchanter r29, Hitchhiker r59,
Trinity r12, Beyond Zork r57, Photopia, Lost Pig, Dr Ludwig,
Counterfeit Monkey 11), both positions, skipping words the story knows:

| typed length | offers / unknown pairs | rate |
|---|---|---|
| **3** (new) | 852 / 11948 | **7.13%** |
| 4 (already shipping) | 26688 / 47560 | 56.11% |
| 5 (already shipping) | 23871 / 97453 | 24.49% |

The band that was silenced is the quietest by a factor of eight. The 56%
at four letters is the NEAR MISS, which `MIN_LEN` never restrained above
its own threshold.

Asked of the meaning table alone (`verb_synonyms::suggest` direct, so
the near miss cannot dominate), Zork I: **7.0% at three, 5.8% at four,
3.3% at five, 1.8% at six**. Same table, slightly denser where words are
more polysemous — not a step change, and the same source that has been
unrestricted at every other length since SQ-1119.

### The sample, read rather than counted

Zork I: 1242 unknown three-letter words, **106 answered**.

* **12 irregular, all correct** — `ate`→eat, `bit`→bite, `dug`→dig,
  `fed`→feed, `got`→get, `hid`→hide, `lit`→light, `men`→man, `ran`→run,
  `sat`→sit, `saw`→see, `won`→win. This is the quest.
* **5 suffix rule, all junk** — `gos`, `his`, `its`, `mes`, `ons`. The
  only rule a three-letter word can reach is stripping `s` off a
  two-letter base. Note `his` fires only in the OPENING position, so
  `take his sword` is silent; a player never opens a command with it.
* **89 meaning table**, roughly half useful (`don`→wear, `hat`→wear,
  `dip`→douse, `sip`→drink·swallow, `rid`→free·untie, `use`→apply,
  `gap`→open, `cry`→shout·yell·scream, `tap`→knock·rap, `jab`→push·poke)
  and half not (`sod`→fuck, `bed`→fuck·set·curse, `fee`→hit·fight·hurt,
  `bet`→count·play·look, `tax`→set·flip·shut, `pee`→make).

**Why `confirm` and not `done`:** the numbers say ship it, and the tests
settle the behaviour. But that last bullet is the register's problem, not
a test's — `sod` → `fuck` is a line lanthorn would print in its own voice,
and it is worth an eye before it is trusted. It is NOT new policy: the
same table produces the same class of answer at four letters and up
today, and the probe (`guidance_probe`, on by default) vets each pick in
a shadow session before the line reads `try instead`. If the verdict is
that the meaning table wants pruning, that is its own quest and it is not
about length.

### What was inverted

* `vocabulary_offer.rs`: `zork1_stays_quiet_on_a_three_letter_irregular`
  → `zork1_answers_a_three_letter_irregular_now_that_length_gates_only_the_near_miss`
  (`lit lamp` → `this story knows — light`), with the old reasoning kept
  as the explanation of what changed and why.
* `vocab.rs`: the `don` → `wear` refusal in
  `the_canonical_meanings_reach_the_word_the_story_holds`, commented "the
  gate is older than this source and is not relaxed for it", is now
  `assert_eq!(… , vec!["wear"])`. `don sword` also left
  `zork1_stays_quiet_where_meaning_reaches_nothing_the_story_implements`
  — it never belonged there, being the one refusal in that list that was
  about LENGTH rather than about the story disposing of a proposal.

New silence cases in both files: a near miss (`take lam`, one keystroke
from the `lamp` right there), a word in no table (`oaf`), and a table hit
the story does not hold (`sum` → `sum up`/`summarize`/`tally`, none of
which Zork has).

### Falsified both ways

* Length test back at the top of `offer`: exactly the four hit cases
  fail, each `left: []` — SQ-1113's reported symptom. Every silence case
  still passes.
* Guard removed from `by_near_miss`: the three silence cases fail, and
  `lit` becomes `light · lift · raise` — the near miss corrupting a
  CORRECT answer, which is a sharper argument for the constant than the
  silence cases are.

Gate 0 (6673 tests across 59 binaries, 6673 passed); clippy
`--workspace --all-targets -D warnings` clean.
