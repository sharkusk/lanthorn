--- note 2026-10-09T14:37:11Z ---

Cause: parse_search (ifdb_search.rs:642) keeps only starRating in SearchHit::star_rating (:211-212), though the reply also has averageRating (the module doc at :31-33 mentions both). The iFiction record keeps the true average (ifiction.rs:232, IfdbExt::average_rating), which the story list shows (picker_ui.rs:3378); the modal shows the star value (ifdb_search_modal.rs:819).
Repro: in the IFDB modal Photopia shows 4.5 and Counterfeit Monkey 5; after a fetch the story list says 4.3 and 4.8.
Ask: add average_rating: Option<f32> to SearchHit, parsed from averageRating, so any host can show the same number everywhere; keep star_rating for star glyphs.
