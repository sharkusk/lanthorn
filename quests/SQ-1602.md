--- note 2026-09-26T17:21:39Z ---

boot_story takes TerminalFacts::min_story_screen (SQ-1596) and seeds a pre-boot screen no smaller than the floor. host::reset::reset_game re-runs the boot-time header setup but has no floor parameter, and the smallest-pane search (min_terminal_size_for_story_floor) is private to host::boot. So a host has to redo that search itself to restart a story like Bureaucracy on a small pane. Wanted: reset_game accepts the same floor (or the stored TerminalFacts) and applies it as boot does. Alternatively, make the search public, so a host's restart and resize use the lib's arithmetic.
