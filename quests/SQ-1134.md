## What SQ-1133 actually found

SQ-1133 was reported as "scope is one level deep". Half of that was true — the
CARRIED half was the player's direct children while the room half had nested to
three since SQ-0678, and that is fixed. **The reported Arthur case is a
different defect and is still live.**

## The specimen

`stories/arthur-r74-s890714.z6`, the churchyard, 12 taps in (blank lines / Enter,
answering `n` to the restore question). The torque is on the GROUND, not carried.

The parser is the oracle and was asked, through the real session:

```
> x torque
The torque is an open neckband made of twisted metal, and it looks like it's
about your size. It ends in two knobs, and imbedded in one of the knobs is a
sliver of crystal that gives off a faint glow.
> x crystal
You gaze intently into the crystal. … the hazy outline of a hint menu …
> x knob        (the same object; one word list)
```

The object tree at that moment:

```
#224 "torque"            [peter/torque/torc]           attrs=[17,25,32,40,42]
  └ #112 "sliver of crystal" [crystal/sliver/knob/knobs] attrs=[7,43,45]
#212 "stone"             [stone/rock]                  attrs=[25,40,42,43,45]
  └ #203 "sword"         [sword/excalibur/hilt/shaft/runes] attrs=[20,33,40,43,45]
      └ #171 "jewels"    [jewel/jewels]                attrs=[28,43]
```

The crystal is at depth **1** under an object the room walk already reaches at
depth 0. Depth is not what excludes it — `WorldModel::shows_contents` is.

## The cause

`WorldModel::discover_at_boot` gives Arthur `container_attr = 40`,
`open_attr = 44`. Attribute **44 is the animate bit**: the black knight, Merlin,
King Lot, the demon, the girl, the dragon, the boar, the kraken, Nimue, the blue
knight, the farmers, the guard, the peasant, the idiot, the raven, the old man,
the caged bird, and yourself.

Arthur's real OPENBIT is attribute **42** — 41 objects, every one of them a
container: the whisky jug, the bottle, the barrel, the tankards, the hole, the
thatch, "behind the gravestone", the chair, the torque, the stone Excalibur is
in, and (as ZIL always does) every actor. The cupboard, the cage and the bag —
Arthur's genuinely shut containers — do NOT carry it; they carry attribute 34,
which is the openable/hinged bit.

`infer_open_attr` rejects 42 at **filter 2** (Jaccard against the container set
> 0.6): 41 of 41 of its holders are containers, the container set is 63, so
41/63 = 0.65. That filter exists to reject Mini-Zork's attribute 18, a
containment CLASS marker at 13/17 = 0.76. Measured verdicts, from the pristine
boot image:

| story | container | candidate | n | inside | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zork I r88 | 19 | **11** (correct) | 12 | 9 | SURVIVES |
| Mini-Zork r34 | 9 | **10** (correct) | 9 | 8 | SURVIVES |
| Mini-Zork r34 | 9 | 18 (class marker) | 15 | 13 | REJ ~= container set |
| Arthur r74 | 40 | **42** (correct) | 41 | 41 | REJ ~= container set |
| Arthur r74 | 40 | 44 (animate) | 32 | 26 | SURVIVES |

## Why loosening the threshold is NOT the fix

Move the bar past 0.65 and 42 survives — and so does 44, which is not rejected
by any filter. Filter 3 (a surface-shaped bit nesting strictly inside) passes for
both, because attribute 41 (23 persons) sits inside each. Two survivors means
filter 5 answers **None**, and `None` means no nesting at all: Arthur would then
lose the sword in the stone from its *here* column and still not show the
crystal. Strictly worse.

## So a new signal is needed, and there are two candidates

Both were measured against the corpus and both would light the crystal AND leak
nothing in Zork I. Neither is implemented; each needs its own inference, and a
wrong inference here fails toward MORE, which is the dangerous direction.

1. **The "not takeable" rule.** A *part* is a fixture; a closed container's
   *contents* are portable. Descend into a non-open holder only for children
   that lack the story's takeable bit. Arthur: crystal #112 and jewels #171 have
   no 32 → listed; the cupboard's bottle, the bag's key all have 32 → skipped.
   Zork I: lunch, garlic, leaflet, water, canary, sceptre, map, gunk, emerald all
   have 17 → every closed container stays shut. Needs a `take_attr` inference,
   and the obvious statistical definitions get it wrong on Arthur (attribute 45
   wins a "children of open holders" vote; 32 is the right answer).

2. **The NDESC rule.** In ZIL a part is marked "do not describe separately",
   because the parent's own description names it. Zork I attribute 14 is that bit
   (59 objects: the stairs, the cliffs, the rainbow, the grating, the tables, the
   altar, the pedestal, the trophy case); Arthur's looks like 43 (75 objects).
   Same result on both stories. Needs an `ndesc_attr` inference, and the one
   obvious signal for it — "the bit every child of the local-globals bucket
   carries" — works on Zork I and fails on Arthur, whose LG bucket children carry
   almost no attributes at all.

## Related

fixed; its commit message carries the same numbers. [[SQ-1042]]'s note that
