--- note 2026-09-26T17:41:00Z ---

When a v4/v5/v7/v8 save state is restored into a narrower screen, the declared width stays at the saved width for good. The status bar is then clipped at the right, even for games that lay it out again from $21 every turn.

Path:
- restore_screen (crates/app/src/session.rs:4876 and :4918) calls note_restored_screen_cols(screen.upper.cols) (session.rs:1835-1841, SQ-0681). That raises boot_screen_cols to the saved upper-window width, and it only ever grows.
- declared_story_screen_dims (render/screen.rs:539-549) floors every later declaration at that value, via host::screen::sync_zvm_screen_dims and loop_tick::poll_zvm_screen_dims.
- Every restore path does this: engine_helpers.rs:356 apply_archive_state, host/boot.rs:1286 auto-resume, and in-game @restore.

The SQ-0681 raise protects games that compute their field columns once at boot (Zork 1 r52 at columns 60/73). A game that reads $21 each time it draws its status line has nothing width-dependent in its memory image. Lost Pig (Inform 6, z8) is one: booted at 60 columns its bar is 'Outside … 0', and one turn after widening to 120 it re-lays itself as 'Outside … Score: 0' at the right edge. For such games the raised floor only hides the right part of the bar.

Headless repro with LostPig.z8:
1. boot_story with TerminalFacts { size: Some((120,30)) }, then set_story_pane((120,30)). Submit 'look', then host::persist::save_state_now.
2. boot_story a second session at (60,30), then set_story_pane((60,30)). $21 is 59.
3. host::persist::restore_file, then set_story_pane((60,30)). Submit 'look' twice.
4. Expected: $21 == 59 and upper.cols == 59. Actual: 119 and 119 (boot_screen_cols 119). A real save made at 130 columns gives 130.

Acceptance:
- After a restore, a game that re-reads the header width during play is told the host's screen again, by the turn after its first redraw at the latest. That's this session's own boot width, grown with the pane as today.
- zork1_restore_status_width.rs still passes.
- The auto-resume and @restore paths behave the same way.

A possible approach: zvm notes whether the game reads $21 (a loadb/loadw covering it) during a turn after its first input. The restore floor is then kept only until such a read is seen. The same signal could also lift the SQ-0679 boot floor for such games when the pane narrows.
