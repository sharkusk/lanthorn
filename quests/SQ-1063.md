## What the capture already settles

* **The flanks are TILED and their source is a full-height crop.** Both flank bands read `[Art, tiled]` with `source 76x1224` / `84x1224` **native px** — 1224 rows of source for a game screen that is only **390 native px tall**. So the tiler is being handed a source three times the height of the artwork and is repeating it to fill; whatever is at native y=0 — which is the **top panel** — comes round again down the column. That is exactly the reported symptom: the reversed section of the side art includes the very top, because the very top IS the top panel.
* **`into 76x1224px at (84,0) of 160x1224`** — the left flank is composed into a surface twice its own width at x-offset 84, the right at x-offset 0 of 168. That mirroring offset is worth reading closely; it is how the two flanks share one tile sheet, and a wrong offset would mirror one of them.
* **The pane is unusually tall for the frame**: 102x68 cells at 8x18 px = 816x1224 device px against a 637x390 native screen. Scale is **1.00** with `y-offset 0`, so the frame occupies the top 390 device rows and the flanks are asked to cover all 1224. The report says "in really long window", which matches — the taller the pane, the more of the column is filler.
* **`blank rows 0, longest run 0 at 0`** on both flanks, so the tiler found no blank seam to cut on and fell back to repeating the whole crop.

## Where to look

`render::v6_layout`'s flank handling and the band tiler in `render::graphics` — specifically how a flank's SOURCE crop is chosen when the destination is taller than the frame. SQ-0779 (`clear_text_columns`) and the SQ-0750 flank rules are the neighbours; `v6_side_border_tiling` is the existing suite and `extend_raster_flanks` the raster twin.

**A flank's source must be the frame's own side column and nothing above it.** The top panel is a separate strip — the dump lists it as `strip:art 61x4 at (21,1)` — and it must not be inside the flank's crop. Check whether the crop starts at native y=0 rather than at the top of the side column proper.

Reproduce on the Macintosh press at a tall pane; `stories/Shogun.toast` is release **292 / serial 890314** (SQ-1055), and name whichever medium the repro uses, since a disk image is a different release rather than the same story on other media. State the pane size and the turn count.
