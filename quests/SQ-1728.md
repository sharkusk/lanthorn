  cover::decode (cover.rs:26) and Blorb picture decoding call image::load_from_memory with the crate defaults (no width/height cap, 512 MiB per decode). graphics.rs (~422-449) cache/scaled_cache/adaptive_* maps grow without a byte bound, so a Blorb of many small-on-disk huge PNGs can use gigabytes; an allocation failure aborts the whole process. Use image::Limits (e.g. 4096x4096, smaller max_alloc) in the shared decode helpers and an LRU byte budget for the caches. Also: V6 native_extent (render/v6_layout.rs:266) can reach 16384x16384 because position is added on top of WINDOW_PX_CAP; hosts that rasterise the canvas would benefit from a documented cap.
