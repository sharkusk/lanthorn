## The misplacement

`zvm::interpreter` is the machine table an embedder needs: §11.1.3 interpreter
numbers (`MACINTOSH_INTERPRETER_NUMBER` and friends), per-machine default colour
pairs (`MAC_DEFAULT_BACKGROUND`/`FOREGROUND`, `AMIGA_*`, `ST_*`, `APPLE_*`),
palettes via `palette_for`, and `PeriodLook`. All of it is Z-machine knowledge
about real hardware, and all of it is in the engine.

**The Version 6 character cell is not.** `InterpreterProfile::v6_font_cell` lives
in `crates/app/src/interpreter.rs`; `zvm` mentions it only in a doc comment on
`V6Cell`. So a host embedding `zvm` is handed the Macintosh's interpreter number
and its white-on-black default pair by the engine, and then has to discover for
itself that the machine declares a **7x15** cell — or, far more likely, use 8x16
and inherit exactly the defect SQ-0917 spent a day fixing.

`colWidth := 7` is not lanthorn presentation policy. It is the same category of
fact as `MAC_DEFAULT_BACKGROUND`, which is already in `zvm`.

## What to move

The cell, and the reasoning with it. SQ-0917 established it four independent
ways off 1:1 captures and one Pascal listing:

- `mac/xzip.lst`: `stdFont := geneva; textSize (12); lineHeight := 15 {16};
  colWidth := 7`
- the inverse PROLOGUE bar in `machine-screenshots/mac-zorkzero-hint.png` is
  exactly 15 rows — and a bar is drawn to the CELL, not to ink
- the topic list indexes as `y = 118 + 15*i` across fourteen rows
- the colour press's prose tops run 266/281/296/311/326/341
- the insertion caret is 1px wide and 15 tall in both colour captures

Carry the **declared-versus-drawn** note too (it is on `V6Cell` already): the
Macintosh DRAWS proportional Geneva and DECLARES 7, and 7 is simply Geneva 12's
average advance. An embedder that reads "7" as a drawn advance will get
proportional rendering wrong in the other direction.

## Shape

`app::InterpreterProfile` should delegate rather than duplicate — it already
re-exports `zvm::interpreter`'s numbers rather than restating them, and
`interpreter_profile`'s agreement test exists to keep the two tables from
drifting. Follow that pattern: the fact lives in `zvm`, `app` asks for it, and
the existing test keeps them honest.

Note `zvm::interpreter::MachineProfile` already exists as the per-machine row —
that is the natural home, not a free function.

## Do NOT move with it

`InterpreterProfile::std_window` and `resolve`/`resolve_with_source`. Those read
the MEDIUM (a mounted disk image, a named archive) and depend on `blorb`, which
`zvm` cannot see and must not — the zero-dependency rule is what makes the crate
embeddable in the first place. The cell is a property of a machine; resolving
which machine a file came from is a host concern.
