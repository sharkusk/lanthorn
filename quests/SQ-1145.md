## Re-scoped 2026-08-30: the NEAR-MISS path is already decided, and correctly. Only the MEANING table is open.

I filed this meaning to extend `hide_adult_words` over the whole offer line. That would have reversed a deliberate, reasoned, already-pinned decision. It should not be done, and the codebase says so in advance.

`crates/app/tests/suites/adult_words.rs` holds two cases:

* **`a_word_hidden_from_the_panel_is_still_offered_by_the_light`**, whose doc names the mistake exactly: *"This is the property a future lane will want to 'unify' away: the band's column and `vocab`'s suggestion both draw on the story's dictionary, so it looks like one filter would do for both. It would not. A word the player reached for is not an unprompted enumeration, and SQ-1115 rules the offer."* It even states the falsification: have `StoryVocabulary::offer` consult `Config::adult_words` and the second half fails.
* **`the_suggestion_path_never_reads_the_adult_list`**, a source-level guard in the spirit of `palette_lock_discipline`: it greps `src/vocab.rs` for `adult_words`, `hide_adult_words` and `hidden_display_words` and fails on any of them, with the message *"Filter the panel, not the offer."*

The distinction is real and worth keeping: the band **enumerates unprompted**; the offer **answers a word you reached for**. Hiding `molest` from a column you did not ask for is a different act from refusing to tell you the story knows it after you typed `molst`.

## What that decision did NOT contemplate

Its worked example is the **near-miss** path: same word, corrected spelling — `molst` → `molest`. That is plainly a word the player reached for, and it must keep working.

`sod` → `fuck` is the **meaning table**, which postdates the decision (SQ-1110, and the three-letter band only became reachable at all with SQ-1144). There the player reached for `sod` and lanthorn proposes a *different* word they never typed. That is nearer to enumeration than to correction, and the pinned reasoning does not reach it.

## The scope, therefore

Filter **only meaning-table proposals** — the `by_meaning` source, `verb_synonyms::suggest` — against `hidden_display_words()`. Leave the near-miss and the story's own synonym paths untouched.

**Do not put the filter in `StoryVocabulary::offer`.** Its having "the story's tables and the typed line, and nothing else to consult" is a deliberate structural property, and the source-level guard exists to keep it. `offer_vocabulary` (`vocab.rs:1290`) holds `state` and therefore the config; that is where a filter belongs, or at a `Config`-level assembly point beside the two that already exist.

Both existing cases must keep passing UNCHANGED. If either has to move, the change is wrong.

## Incidental, fix while in there

`config.rs:1792` cites the guard as `adult_words_filter_every_assembly`. No such test exists; the real one is `every_verb_table_in_src_is_assembled_through_config` in the same suite. A comment pointing at a test that is not there is the SQ-1105 defect class in miniature.
