--- note 2026-09-26T19:25:11Z ---

pager.rs (~352-386): when a turn's output lands with the reader at the bottom, the pager arms arm_transcript_follow_ease(added, target), whatever happened to the transcript that turn. After the story clears its screen (erase_window on the lower window, or the post-clear top-anchor, as at Anchorhead's opening), the new screen scrolls into view from where the old one was. It should simply appear, top-anchored, the way a cleared screen always has. Easing only makes sense when output is appended below what the reader was already looking at.

Wanted: no follow ease on a turn that cleared the story window (or reset the clear anchor). Place the view at its target instantly, as before SQ-1595. Appended output keeps the ease. Hosts that run the lib's follow decision inherit this automatically.

Test: a story that clears and prints a new screen (Anchorhead's opening, or a synthetic erase_window) arms no Follow ScrollAnim, while a normal appended turn still does.
