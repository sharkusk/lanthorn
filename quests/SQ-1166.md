## Two problems, and the second is the reason to change the shot rather than repair it

**1. The frame is captured on a `[more]` pager** — the defect its own manifest comment
was written to prevent. `macintosh-embedded-face` ends:

    You are standing at the end of a road before a
                                            [more]

against a comment beside its recipe reading "the two after it clear the `[more]`
pager the credits raise, because a pager eats whatever key comes next and **a still
of a pager is not a still of a typeface**". The pager was not cleared and the
recipe's `text:look` was almost certainly eaten by it, so the frame shows the credits
block and a truncated first line where a settled page of prose was intended.

**2. The subject is wrong** (user, 2026-08-31): *"that's not a mac game, and really
isn't graphical. we should show the mac render in an real mac z6 game."*

The shot's subject today is the **fallback** face — what lanthorn draws when a bare
story file is TOLD it is a Macintosh (`--interpreter 3` on `advent.z6`) and there is
no Macintosh disk to supply a face, so it falls back to X11 misc-fixed 7x14. That is
a real behaviour and the caption explains it well, but it is an edge case
demonstrated on a game that is neither Macintosh nor graphical, occupying a slot in
a gallery whose job is to show what lanthorn does with real presses.

## What to show instead

A REAL Macintosh v6 press, rendering the Mac's own embedded face at its own 7x15
cell. Available:

| press | release | used by a shot today |
|---|---|---|
| `Zork Zero Disk.image` | r296/881019 | `zork0-mac-mono`, `zork0-mac-tall` |
| `InfocomMasterpieces.img` | r296 — the same build | — |
| **`Shogun.toast`** | **r292/890314** | **nothing** |

**`Shogun.toast` is the natural choice.** It is a genuine Mac v6 press, it is
graphical, no shot uses it, it was only pinned in the release table an hour ago
(SQ-1158, after the user corrected the claim that Shogun had no Macintosh pressing),
and it PAIRS with `shogun-amiga-extended` — the same game on two machines is the
comparison a gallery does best. Zork Zero's Mac press already carries two shots.

Whoever takes this should confirm the press actually renders its own face rather
than assuming: `InterpreterProfile::release_face_space` / `system_face_space` decide
where a face comes from, and the Macintosh answers `Native` both ways (one native
pixel per face pixel, whatever `art_scale` is). If the Mac Shogun turns out to carry
no release face, say so — that changes what the shot can claim.

## Keep the caption's insight, or move it

The current caption carries a genuinely good fact worth not losing: the built-in VGA
face is drawn for an EIGHT-pixel advance and inks 76 of its 94 printable glyphs out
to column 6, so a 7-wide Macintosh cell drops the column holding the letters apart —
which is why the fallback is misc-fixed 7x14 rather than VGA. If the fallback shot
goes, that belongs in `docs/features/interpreter.md` or beside
`native_font::resolve`, not deleted with the entry.

## And apply the guard

`expect_prose_cells` (SQ-1164) is a floor on alphanumeric cells inside the story
pane, and it would have failed this frame — a truncated line plus a pager is far
below a settled page. It was not applied here because SQ-1164 was scoped to the
Journey shots the user had flagged. **This is the third gallery recipe found wrong
today** (five Journey frames stopping at a menu, and this), all the same shape: a
recipe written against an assumption about how many keys reach a frame, with nothing
checking that the frame arrived. Apply the guard to this shot, and consider whether
every prose-bearing shot should carry it rather than the ones someone happened to
look at.

## Do not

Delete any other shot. Gallery curation remains an open editorial question the user
has not settled.
