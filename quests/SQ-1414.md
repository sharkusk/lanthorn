LICENCE BLOCK + specimen catalogue (lane report, 2026-09-09).

Gargoyle's terps/scott/ is GPL-2+ IN ITS ENTIRETY — no permissive subdirectory. Evidence: garglk License.txt line 32 "ScottFree is copyright (c) Alan Cox. It is released under the terms of the GNU General Public License"; terps/scott/scott.c carries the GPL-2-or-later header; every other file there (detectgame.c, scottgameinfo.c, parser.c, ti994a/*, saga/*, ai_uk/*) heads itself "part of ScottFree". ai_uk/sagadraw.c ("Sagadraw v2.5, originally by David Lodge") carries NO licence of its own, so it inherits the aggregate GPL and is equally off-limits. Nothing under that path is usable by a BSD-3-Clause crate.

THIS LANE IS DISQUALIFIED FROM IMPLEMENTING THE LOADERS. Before the licence correction arrived I had already read, in full or near-full: ti994a/load_ti99_4a.c (entire, 572 lines), ti994a/ti99_4a_terp.c (entire, 549 lines), detectgame.c (lines 1-1200 of 1516 — dictKeys, GetId, ReadHeader, SanityCheckHeader, ReadDictionary, SeekIfNeeded, ParseHeader, TryLoadingOld, TryLoading, IsMysterious), scottdefines.h struct GameInfo + the result enums, scottgameinfo.c lines 1-80 (two games[] entries) and sysdict_TI994A, and ~350 scattered lines of scott.c (Look, PerformActions incl. its TI-99 branch, PerformLine tail, PrintMessage, PlayerIsDead, PrintScore, the item/room/counter helpers, the main loop). So TI-99/4A (loader AND bytecode), snapshot extraction, the compressed action table and the gameinfo table shape must all go to a fresh implementer. NOT read: saga/*.c bodies, ai_uk/decompresstext.c, ai_uk/decompressz80.c, c64decrunch.c, apple2detect.c, atari8detect.c, parser.c bodies — so SAGA graphics and the .z80/C64 decompressors are still open to an unexposed implementer.

WHAT LANDED (22620db7): detection re-attributed to measurement, no behaviour change. Doc comments now describe each signature as the game's own verb dictionary opening AUTO,GO in fixed-width slots — 4/5/6-byte fields for word lengths 3/4/5, and the packed variant that drops the NUL pad and marks synonyms by upper-casing the first letter instead of prefixing '*' (gO canonical, CLIM/ENTE synonyms). The old comment's claim that the packed form uses "the high bit of alternating bytes" is wrong and is gone. Nine committed fixture tests + crates/scott/tests/dialect_specimens.rs (real corpus, skips loudly).
