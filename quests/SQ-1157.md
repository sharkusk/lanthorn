--- note 2026-08-30T17:59:00Z ---

REPRO (user, Arthur): type `was`. The parser answers "Sorry, but I don't understand. Please rephrase that, or try something else." — long enough that the bottom status band WRAPS TO TWO LINES. The trigger is the band's HEIGHT changing, not the text.

Per mode:
- HYBRID — the side art switches to RASTER-mode size and overwrites the hybrid ring. Restores on the next command.
- RASTER — correct throughout. (Expected: raster geometry is already what the others are collapsing to.)
- EXTENDED — drops back to raster size until the next command, i.e. loses the surplus height that is the whole point of the mode (SQ-1032).

READ OF IT, to be checked not assumed: all three symptoms are one fact — when the bottom band grows a row, the frame is re-laid-out and comes back on the RASTER baseline rather than the mode currently in force. Raster looks fine because raster is the baseline being fallen back to. That it self-heals on the next command says the mode is still recorded correctly somewhere; the recompute simply is not reading it.

WHERE TO LOOK FIRST: `render::v6_layout::FrameGeometry` and whatever recomputes it on a chrome-height change. This is the refactoring-policy shape described in CLAUDE.md — facts that must be considered together travelling separately, so a recompute that omits one gets a PLAUSIBLE answer rather than an error. SQ-1022 is the closest precedent: `reset.rs` re-derived the boot facts instead of carrying them and re-booted on a different grid.

RELATED, not confirmed as the quest the user had in mind: SQ-1076 (Shogun's status band overrunning the side ornaments, and its note about the extended flank meaning artwork gets overwritten) and SQ-0500 (text-only chrome bands painted as terminal cells in hybrid — Arthur's status bar is one of its two oracles). I could not positively identify an earlier "extended screen shrinking" quest by search; if one exists, link it.

NON-VACUITY, for whoever writes the test: assert the frame SHAPE at both band heights, and assert it in all three modes — a case pinned only to raster cannot fail on any of this.
