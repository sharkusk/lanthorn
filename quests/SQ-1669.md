--- note 2026-10-02T00:35:39Z ---

Investigation 2026-10-01 (read-only, Opus fork).

1. Tidy does NOT fix it. After relayout_auto, 78 stays at (-3,-3) and 180 at (-6,-1). This is a tidy bug, not an incremental-placement one (incremental never moves placed rooms, incremental.rs:16, by design).

2. Root cause. After the stress stage 78 is correctly west of 180. Contiguify's eject_interlopers (layout/mod.rs:610, called at :1308) then ejects 180 off the Living Room-Kitchen-Behind House row (193-203-79), where its N/S claims to 81/80 had put it. The best-scoring exit (:655) moves 180 west along that row. That is geographically right, but it breaks 180 W 78. 78 is a member of the 78-75-77-239 E/W chain, and chain members are never moved (:600), so nothing corrects it. Constraint tiering (constraints.rs:198-219) and the distorted flag are not involved.

3. The house ring is self-contradictory (180 N 81 vs 81 W 180), so not every hint can hold. The 78 claim is achievable, though: moving 78 alone to (-7,-3) keeps the 78-75 row (SQ-1376: a cardinal claims a line at any length), collides with nothing, and raises directional_hint_score from 1674 to 1723.

Proposed fix: a contiguify sub-step after tighten_runs. When an end member of a run violates a one-way bearing on the run's own axis, slide it outward along its line through free cells only. Risks: stretched passages, and tighten_runs pulling it back unless it respects the bearing. Must stay green: sq1376_cardinal_line, sq1364_clearing_gap, zork1_maze, walkthrough, SQ-1637 frame-count tests.

Fixture: commit the specimen map.json as unit_tests/zork1_r88_late_forest.json (precedent: zork1_walked_map.json). Assert x(78) < x(180) after relayout, and 78/75 share a row. Add a synthetic miniature in the sq1376 style.

Order independence (maintainer asked about placing rooms in room-number order): tidy is already order-independent for this map. Five insertion orders gave identical positions. Rooms are in a BTreeMap keyed by id (graph.rs:369), seq is never read, and the seed comes from sort_layout, not stored positions. The one leak is connection Vec order, used as a same-tier tie-break (constraints.rs:219 → creates_cycle drop choice → vpsc.rs:51). Filed separately.

--- note 2026-10-02T00:45:02Z ---

Re-investigation through the app's real /tidy (run_tidy_pipeline, input.rs:2489/2541; background tidy tidy_layer_silent, tidy.rs:352).

A. Same graph, permuted room/connection/seq order: identical final positions under every permutation. replay_build_and_placement's positions are never read by relayout_auto_observed, which reseeds from sort::sort_layout.

B. Six real walk orders replayed through Mapper::observe (including reversed, with 180 W 78 walked first): same 48-edge set, identical tidied positions.

So the order-dependence the maintainer sees most likely comes from different explorations recording different EDGE SETS, not from the order itself. Tidy is highly sensitive to the edge set: removing any one of the 48 edges flips Forest(78) to west of 180 in 13 cases, several of them far away (End of Rainbow SW, Canyon View NW, Kitchen/Living Room). The global all-pairs stress solve (mod.rs:1566-1570) shifts what contiguify's eject picks.

The Forest bug reproduces under every ordering; root cause and proposed fix unchanged. SQ-1671 (cardinal over diagonal in collapse_stacked_exits) is bundled into this fix lane by maintainer decision.
