## Confirmed (user, 2026-08-27) — and it explains a symptom that predates the quest

> "i do remember having a one or two line issue with lock on previously, so this seems
> to solve that issue"

That is independent corroboration of the mechanism, and the arithmetic matches: the
small-pad cases ARE one-or-two-line artifacts.

## What the padding actually is: the thing that makes the pixel lock hold

Not "better fractional upscaling", which is what the title says and what it does NOT
deliver (see below). The honest description is **the pixel lock now holds on cells
that do not divide the composite.**

The lock's whole promise is that every art pixel is the same whole number of device
pixels. It delivers that, and then the placement's cell rect rounds UP and the
terminal stretches the composite to fill it — breaking the guarantee in the last step
of the chain, after the lock did its job perfectly. At a 16x32 cell and s=3 the
composite is 1920x1200 with every art pixel exactly 3 device rows; the placement is
1216 rows; the stretch makes **16 of those art rows 4 device pixels instead of 3**.

Whether it bites at all depends entirely on the CELL, for a 640x400 press:

| cell | s=1 | s=2 | s=3 | s=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16x32 | pad 0x16 | clean | pad 0x16 | clean |
| 8x16 | clean | clean | clean | clean |
| 8x18 | pad 0x14 | pad 0x10 | pad 0x6 | **pad 0x2** |
| **7x15 (Macintosh)** | pad 4x5 | **pad 1x10** | pad 5x0 | pad 2x5 |
| 9x20 | pad 8x0 | pad 7x0 | pad 6x0 | pad 5x0 |

On 8x16 or 10x20 it does nothing, ever. On 8x18, 7x15 or 9x20 it does something at
every rung — and 7x15 is the Macintosh cell. A terminal's cell comes from its font, so
which column a player is in is not something lanthorn chooses.

**Judge this change with `/set-v6-pixel-lock on`, in raster, at an ODD rung** — not by
looking for smoother fractional scaling.

## The titled complaint is NOT fixed, and is now SQ-1099

"Better upscaling quality when the pixel lock is off and the magnification is
fractional" is untouched. Under Nearest at a fractional scale the art pixels of one
frame get different widths — measured over a 640-wide press:

| s | composite | art-pixel widths |
|---|---|---|
| 1.25 | 800px | 1px x480, 2px x160 |
| **1.5** | 960px | **1px x320, 2px x320** |
| 1.9 | 1216px | 1px x64, 2px x576 |
| 2.5 | 1600px | 2px x320, 3px x320 |

At 1.5x, half the strokes in the frame are one device pixel and half are two. That 2:1
variation is what makes a fractional magnification look interpolated, and what shipped
here removed a ~1.3% terminal resample sitting beside it — real, and a rounding error
by comparison. Split to SQ-1099 rather than left parked on a closed quest.
