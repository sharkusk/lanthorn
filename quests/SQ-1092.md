## Reproduced, and the panel is CLIPPED as well as off-centre

`stories/zork0-r393-s890714.z6` (Zork Zero, release 393 / serial 890714), 82x34
kitty, 640x400 native at 2.000x, four keypresses: `cr`, `look`, then `ctrl+p`.

The leader panel lands low and right — its top border is at roughly row 26 of 34
— and its bottom edge and the `Done` button run off the pane entirely. So this is
not only a centring complaint: at this size part of the dialog is unreachable by
eye, and a modal that cannot show its own buttons is worse than one that is
merely off-centre.

The command palette (`ctrl+p` then `/`) shows the same placement.

## What it looks like from here

Dropping to text-only for the modal is correct and necessary — the dialog is
drawn with glyphs, so the pixel ring has to come off to make room. What does not
follow is the placement: whatever rect the dialog is centred in appears to be the
one the v6 frame left behind (the story VIEWPORT inside the ring) rather than the
pane it now has to itself once the art is gone.

That would explain both symptoms at once. The v6 story viewport is inset from the
pane on all four sides and sits low, so centring in it puts the dialog low and
right relative to the whole pane, and a dialog sized for the PANE then overruns
the bottom of the smaller rect it was placed in.

## Where to look

`overlays::draw_overlays` takes a `dialog_area`, and every common dialog centres
itself in it via `Placement::Centered`. The question is what `dialog_area` is on
a v6 frame — find where it is derived in `render::screen` / `draw_frame` and
whether the v6 path narrows it to the story viewport. If so the fix is to hand
the modals the full pane once the frame is suppressed, since suppressing the
frame is precisely what frees that space.

Worth checking the non-v6 case at the same size to confirm the dialog is centred
there — that comparison is what says this is v6-specific rather than a general
placement bug.
