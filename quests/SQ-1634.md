--- note 2026-09-28T14:57:16Z ---

crates/app/src/host/boot.rs ~1852-1853 (the SQ-1629 Fix 2 boot drain) calls apply_room_description and apply_item_observations with state.turns, but state.turns is only restored from the save's turn count later, at ~1924-1925 (if let Some(turns) = startup_turns). On a fresh boot that's right (turn 0). On a resume it stamps every item re-observed in the room the player resumes in, and that room's description, with turn 0 instead of the save's turn, so a host shows 'seen at move 0' and loses the real last-seen turn.
Repro: Zork I r88, open mailbox, take leaflet, (six moves in West of House), save/exit, resume: the mailbox and front door come back with last-seen turn 0 instead of 6; items elsewhere are unaffected.
Ask: record the boot drain's observations with the restored turn (restore state.turns before the drain on a resume, or pass startup_turns.unwrap_or(state.turns)).
Test: resume a save whose starting room has items and a description; their turns equal the save's turn count.
