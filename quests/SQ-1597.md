--- note 2026-09-26T14:46:59Z ---

SQ-1595 added the story pane's follow ease (animation.follow_ms). The lib decides when a follow applies: only when the reader was at the newest text, only up to a [more] park, and cancelled by input. It arms state.scroll_anim. A host that renders the pane itself can see scroll_anim, but it can't tell a follow ease from a scroll_ms ease (paging or catching up), so it has to restate the follow rule to animate the same way.

Wanted: a public way for a host to learn, per turn or frame, that the lib armed a FOLLOW ease, with its from and to offsets (or the target row: the [more] park or the end), plus the duration and easing to use. For example a kind field on ScrollAnim (Follow | Scroll), or a host-facing FollowEase { to_row, ms, easing } on the turn result. Hosts then run the lib's decision instead of restating it.
