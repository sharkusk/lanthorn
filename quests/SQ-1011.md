## The symptom, and the measurement behind it

Reported by eye the moment SQ-0917 landed: **Macintosh raster text looks worse
than the Amiga's.** It does, and the cause is not the cell being wrong — the cell
is right. It is that `render/vga16.rs` is an **8-wide face being drawn at a 7-wide
advance**, and the pixel that disappeared is the tracking, not the letter.

Census of the table over printable ASCII (0x21..0x7E), by rightmost inked column
in the 8-wide master:

| rightmost inked column | glyphs | examples |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1 | `'` |
| 4 | 6 | `, . : ; \` \|` |
| 5 | 9 | `! ( ) I [ ] f i l` |
| **6** | **76** | `" # $ % & + - / 0 1 2 3 4 5 …` |
| 7 | 2 | `* _` |

**78 of 94 reach column 6 or 7.** In an 8-wide cell their ONLY right-side gap is
column 7 — that column is the inter-character space. `blit_glyph_styled` resamples
`dx * 8 / cw`, so at `cw == 7` it samples columns 0..=6 and drops column 7
entirely: those glyphs now fill the cell edge to edge and touch their neighbours.

Vertically nothing is lost. `dy * 16 / 15` maps rows 0..=14 one-for-one and never
reaches row 15, and no glyph in the table inks row 15. **The defect is purely
horizontal crowding.**

Hybrid is unaffected — it draws terminal glyphs and never touches this face.

## The fix is already parsed, and its stated blocker is gone

`native_disk_font.rs::the_macintosh_font_is_fixed_pitch_but_narrower_than_our_cell`
pins the shipped face off `stories/Zork Zero Disk.image`:

- `FONT` 524, **7x15**, baseline 12, 200+ glyphs, covering Mac roman
- **fixed-pitch at 7** across printable ASCII
- carries left side bearings, which that case notes are load-bearing: previewing
  without them "flushed every glyph left and made evenly-advanced text look
  raggedly letter-spaced"

That is EXACTLY the cell SQ-0917 now declares, so it would blit **1:1 with no
resampling on either axis** — which is the property `vga16` was introduced to
provide (SQ-0932) and is precisely what it cannot deliver at 7 wide.

And the same case names the obstacle, which no longer exists:

> "So the obstacle to drawing with these glyphs is not the font. It is that using
> them properly needs the font's METRICS as well, which is a change to what
> `$26`/`$27` tell the game and moves every window the game lays out. Pinned so
> that stays measured rather than remembered."

SQ-0917 made that change. `$26`/`$27` say 15 and 7, every window is laid out from
them, and the gate is green. **The blocker SQ-0916 recorded has been removed by
SQ-0917, and this quest is what that unlocks.**

## A correction this carries

SQ-0917's own notes argue "the Macintosh needs the CELL and not the glyphs", and
that wiring `FONT` 524 into the renderer "would be wrong on its own terms, because
the real interpreter did not tell the story about proportional widths either."

**The first half stands and the second does not.** For the HEADER, the declared
metric is the whole story and 7 is right regardless of how anything is painted.
But the RASTER path still has to put an actual bitmap in the cell, and a face
drawn for an 8-pixel advance has nowhere to keep its tracking in a 7-pixel one.
Which bitmap to draw and what to declare are two questions, and that note ran them
together.

## Scope

- **Raster only.** Hybrid draws terminal glyphs by design.
- **Macintosh only.** Every other machine keeps the 8x16 cell where `vga16` blits
  1:1 already, and must not change.
- The face has to come from the mounted medium, so the fallback when there is no
  disk in hand (a bare `.z6`, CI with `stories/` absent) is still `vga16` — with
  the crowding, which is better than no glyphs. Worth deciding whether a bare
  Macintosh-profiled story should keep the 7x15 cell at all in that case.
- Left side bearings must be applied, per the pinned case above.
- Font 3 (character graphics) stays on `bitfont`'s 8x8 masters; `FONT` 524 is a
  typeface and carries none of it.
