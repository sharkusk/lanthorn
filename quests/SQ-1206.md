### Findings, ranked
1. City of Secrets (CoS.blb, I7 6G25, Glulx 2.0.0) has a UNICODE dictionary (DICT_CHAR_SIZE=4); gvm/src/grammar.rs:206 refuses it (`GrammarError::UnicodeDictionary`, comment says "No story in the corpus uses one" — now false). story_vocabulary() is None → offer, word reveal and command-band verb column are all dark for the whole game. Only total failure in the set.
2. `hasten north` → "try instead — fasten · attach · tie" fires on 17/30 stories. A one-keystroke neighbour outranks hasten's own semantic group (line 308 hie/race/rush/speed). Vetting can't catch it because the object is a DIRECTION: control pair `fasten <absent noun>` → "can't see any such thing" vs `fasten north` → "You would achieve nothing by that" (read as success). Fixes: rank semantic group above keystroke neighbour; add a direction word to the control set.
3. `shove` → "pull · drag · push": pull-group (line 491) precedes push-group (line 809); corpus says 6 stories declare press/push/shove vs 4 drag/pull/shove/tug/yank. Order game-derived groups by support count (fixes the class).
4. `illuminate` → "light · clear · burn" (line 1421, the clarify sense; `burn X` destroys X in most games). Only line 397 (light/light up/illuminate) should reach a parser.
5. un- prefix family reaches nothing: unlink (Savoir-Faire's core mechanic; Tangle), unbuckle, unzip, sheathe/unsheathe, unpin, unsnag, unhide, unmask. `link` itself present in 7 stories. A build-time un-X derivation rule.
6. `extinguish` reaches no group on 5 stories; corpus now declares extinguish/snuff on 3 (one of the 57 promotable groups).
7. Rank `examine` first in its own group (every `inspect` offer read "watch · check · examine"); likewise obtain → get first (find is a distinct action).
8. Story-specific lexicons to recognise and stay quiet about: gostak, suvehnux (lumos/alohomora/zeezee…), Junior Arithmancer (nums/prefixes/slu/oma), oppositely-opal (enfuego/intensus/leviupup), Absence of Law (aud/abt/hyd/kro). A verb table mostly outside English is a signal, not a hole.
9. Meta-verbs treated as ordinary misses: direction/dirs (3), walkthrough/walkthru (4), topics (5), type (4), spells (3), tasks, landmarks, scenes/rules.
10. Laughter/interjection verbs absent: chuckle, giggle, chortle, guffaw; sneeze (3), whisper, behold, shatter (3), straighten (3), incinerate, headbutt, jemmy, wag/yip/woof/slobber (Toby's Nose).
