Cause: ObjectWordSet::build (grammar-model/src/lib.rs:901-917) excludes only articles (:815) and a short preposition list (:863). Inform's compass objects (up/u, out/outside) and I7 group objects (he/him, she/her) pass through reveal::arm / arm_from_text, breaking the module doc's 'never west' promise (reveal.rs:70-76).
Repro: Toby's Nose opening text lights up, out and he; any Inform story lights north, west and so on.
Ask: exclude compass/direction objects, and add personal pronouns (he, him, she, her, it, them, you, me) to the stop set.
