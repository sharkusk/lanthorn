--- note 2026-10-09T14:24:36Z ---

Repro: Photopia (photopia.z5), answer no, then wait three times, pressing a key at each pause. The game shows a short splash (a 6-row upper window, red text on black), then clears (erase_window -1) and starts the Mars scene in red on black.

Expected: a clear acts like a new page. Everything before it stays in the scrollback above, out of view. The view starts at the first line after the clear and is filled with the new background, so only the new red text shows and none of the earlier white-background scene is visible.

Actual: the TUI shows the issue, and any host that renders the transcript sees the same thing. After the clear, the view resets to the start of the story instead of to the clear point. A band of the earlier white background (about 3 rows) also shows above the first Mars line.

Lead for the band: tracing the transcript at 80x30 shows the clear at line 57. Lines 57-59 are blank and have no style runs, so they carry no background colour. Every line from 60 ('You are Wendy Mackaye...') has a black-background, red-text run. Any renderer drawing those blank lines falls back to its default ground (white here) rather than the current background. Hosts need those post-clear blank lines, or the clear point itself, to carry the background colour that was active when erase_window ran. At 80x30 the pager also raised [more] right after this clear (scroll 4), which may be where the jump to the start of the story comes from.
