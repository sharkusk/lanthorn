## Why one mechanism and not two

SQ-1033 measured render cost growing with TOTAL scrollback on all three engines,
in every mode. The two render paths get there by different routes and the routes
converge on the same cure.

**The cell path (which hybrid's story text also uses) has a cache, and it is a
whole-PRODUCT cache that misses every turn.** `TranscriptWrapKey` includes `gen`
and `len`, and `bump_transcript_gen` fires on every transcript mutation — so each
turn invalidates the lot and re-wraps the whole history. That is why hybrid idles
flat at 1.7 ms from 100 turns to 20,000 while its COLD render goes 2.0 -> 17.9 ms.
The hit path looks reassuring and hides an O(total) miss path.

**The raster path has no cache at all** and sits behind a whole-canvas gate whose
key hashes `state.input.value` (`screen.rs:4165`), so a single keystroke on an
unchanged transcript costs a full re-wrap — 25.058 ms measured at 20,000 turns.

## The split the existing key already implies

`TranscriptWrapKey`'s fields divide cleanly, and the division IS the design:

* **append-compatible** — `gen`, `len`. Content only grew. Extend the wrapped
  rows; wrap the new logical lines and nothing else.
* **layout-invalidating** — `width`, `filter`, `images_enabled`, `char_px`,
  `clear_anchor`, `room_name`, `colors`. Any change drops the product and rebuilds.

So "recompute on resize" is really "recompute when a layout field moves"; a resize
is just the common one. Note `clear_anchor` moving is the TRUNCATION case
(`transcript.truncate` at a clear-screen anchor and at a rewind), which this
handles for free by rebuilding rather than needing its own path.

**Raster is the degenerate case, not a second design.** Its `cols` come from
`story_prose_box` over the NATIVE v6 screen rect (`screen.rs:3970`), i.e. the
game's own coordinate space — so `width` is constant for the session and a resize
only rescales the finished composite. Raster therefore lands on the append branch
essentially always. Hybrid and the cell path wrap to the TERMINAL's columns and
take the rebuild branch on resize. Same mechanism, different field volatility.

**And the input line is not part of the wrapped product at all** —
`build_main_text` takes it as a separate argument — so a keystroke becomes FREE
rather than merely cached, once raster stops rebuilding the world for one.

## Why this is a type and not two careful call sites

The defect exists BECAUSE the cache lives in one path and not the other and the
two drifted with nobody noticing. CLAUDE.md's refactoring policy names that shape
exactly: a hand-maintained invariant across files is the symptom, and the cure is
a type. Do not fix raster by copying the cell path's cache into it — that
reproduces the divergence with a second copy. One owner, both callers.

## What it must still handle

* **Floats.** `reserve_at(&floats, wrapped.len())` narrows the rows beside an
  inline picture, so a row's width depends on float positions in WRAPPED
  coordinates. Floats are pushed as content is wrapped, so this looks append-only
  — VERIFY it rather than assuming, because a float that changed position would
  retroactively change earlier rows' widths and quietly corrupt the appended tail.
* **Per-character styles.** `transcript_runs` and the `row_bits` slicing are
  parallel to the wrapped rows and must append in step.
* **The five parallel vectors.** `transcript`, `_styles`, `_runs`, `_para`,
  `_images` are `resize`d on every push and must stay aligned with the wrapped
  product.

## Acceptance

Re-run `cargo run --release -p app --example scroll_bench` (SQ-1033's instrument,
commit `c91d73ab`) before and after. **Both `cold_ms` AND the keystroke cost must
stop growing with turn count**, on all three engines and in both v6 modes. A fix
that flattens one and leaves the other is half a fix, and the harness reports them
separately so there is no hiding it.

Note the harness's own trap, recorded on SQ-1033: the raster path must be polled
with `poll_v6_encode_job` in `main.rs`'s order or it looks flat and cheap for a
false reason.
