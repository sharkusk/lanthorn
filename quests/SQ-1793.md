--- note 2026-10-17T05:19:19Z ---

Details: crates/app/Cargo.toml has zip = "2" (resolves to 2.4.2). The three advisories concern ZipArchive::extract / ZipStreamReader::extract (symlink targets outside the destination, permissions applied through symlinks, SUID/SGID bits kept); crates/app only writes zips (ZipWriter) and reads entries itself (ifdb_documents.rs), so nothing here is exploitable, but cargo audit fails on the version for every dependent, including any embedding host that runs cargo audit in CI. zip 9 renamed or changed some writer APIs (SimpleFileOptions, start_file); check picker_ui.rs and ifdb_documents.rs and their tests. A tag after the bump lets dependents pick it up.
