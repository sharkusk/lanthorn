--- note 2026-10-02T21:17:59Z ---

Draft design (coordinator, 2026-10-02), pending web-agent requirements:
- The right-hand pane (today: map, with the room dock carved off its bottom; crates/app/src/layout.rs) becomes the Journal, with tabs Map · Room · Inventory · Hints · Documents.
- The story/Journal splitter, its drag and the story-only / journal-only / split layouts stay ("map" becomes "journal").
- Retired, with no back-compat shims (pre-release policy): the full-width inventory dock (Boundary::InvDockTop, inv_dock_pct) and the room dock (Boundary::RoomDockTop, room_dock_pct), along with their config/style keys.
- Tab switching:
  - click the tab labels;
  - a `journal-tab <name>` command in slash::COMMANDS (bindable);
  - default keys Alt+1..5 if free (verify against keymap.rs). Not plain Tab, which the input line's autocomplete owns.
- Persist the active tab and the pane width.
- Every new element is styleable (ColorScheme + style.rs + template): tab bar, active and inactive tab.
- v1 mirrors the web: one tab visible at a time. Docking a second tab under the Map on tall screens is deferred.
- Build order: this quest (Map/Room/Inventory tabs, plus placeholder Hints/Documents only if useful), then SQ-1681 Documents, then Hints.
- Open: what the web's Hints tab shows (Invisiclues hint files vs the Guiding Light vocabulary assist), and exact tab labels/order matching the web.
- Interacts with SQ-1271 (Command Panel compass rose) and existing room-dock/inventory-dock suites, which need rewriting.
