Repro: hitchhiker-invclues-r31-s871119.z5 (Infocom Solid Gold, V5, 9-character dictionary). Play "turn on light", "get up", "get gown". The gown becomes a child of object #202 ("it", parse word "protagoni"), the real avatar. The lib locks #19 ("yourself", a global object parented to #58, not a room) as the player, so the carried set stays empty and the gown is recorded as ItemLocation::Vanished { room: 150, turn: 3 }. In hitchhiker-r59-s851108.z3 the same steps lock #31 ("protag") and the gown is Carried.

Two faults:
1. crates/zvm/src/location.rs:906: PLAYER_WORDS has "protag" (PROTAGONIST cut to V3's 6 characters). V5 stores "protagoni" (9), and ObjectWords::refers_to (crates/grammar-model/src/lib.rs:745-748) cuts the query to the object's word length, so "protag" never matches "protagoni". Fix: list "protagonist" in full (cut to the story's own resolution) or match by prefix.
2. crates/zvm/src/location.rs:811-818 (find_player_object_with): with fewer than two candidates, or one that has a parent, it returns that candidate without checking it is in (or carried into) the player's current room, contrary to the doc around :845 and :900 saying consumers validate against the room. Here the lone candidate #19 sits under #58 and is locked anyway.

Where it sticks: crates/app/src/host/turn.rs:792-799 locks state.player_obj once from player_object(); turn.rs:817 passes it back as the hint; zvm_item_observations (crates/app/src/session.rs:6826-6863) then walks #19 every turn.

Effect for any host and the TUI: the inventory view never shows carried items in this story, and anything that relies on "the player now carries X" (e.g. judging whether a take succeeded) never fires.

Tests wanted: in the z5, "get gown" leaves the gown Carried; the z3 unchanged.
