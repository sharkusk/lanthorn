## The report

Enter Arthur's F5 description screen, leave it, and **all the game text comes back
coloured rather than white**. Amiga press, raster and hybrid.

## Established: the engine is not holding a colour

The SQ-1009 engine-pen lane checked this before returning it. At every point —
before F5, inside F5, after leaving F5, and after the next `look` — `zvm` reports
`current_fg` and `current_bg` as `Default`, and **every window** as `fg=Default
bg=Default colour_data=0x0000`. So no `set_colour` is outstanding and nothing in
the engine's colour state explains it.

## Established: not a SQ-1009 regression

Reproduced identically with `TextFace` forced to hand zvm a `V6Metric::fixed`,
which is the pre-SQ-1009 engine exactly: same end state, `attrs=1001`, 29 painted
runs, all colours `Default`. So it is present at `ee962412` and older.

## The lead

**`win2` stays `attrs=1001` with its painted runs after F5 exits.**

F5 turns win2 — normally the PICTURE window — into a text window: attributes go
`1000 -> 1001` and the model goes `Graphics -> Grid` (both captures are in
`~/.lanthorn/dump-windows.log`, timestamps `22:51:27` and `22:51:33`). Leaving F5
does not appear to take it back, so a window that should be graphics again is
still a grid full of retired text runs, and the renderer is colouring text against
whatever that window's state implies.

So look **downstream of the window-kind change and at paint lifetime**, not at
colour state:

* what, if anything, is supposed to return win2 to `attrs=1000` on leaving F5 —
  the game, or us reading it wrongly;
* whether win2's painted runs are being retired when it stops being a text window
  (v6 text is PAINT: a run stays where it was put until painted over or erased —
  `V6Windows::paint_run` / `erase_screen_rect`);
* which layer actually chooses the ink for the story text once win2 is in that
  state, since the engine is handing it `Default`.

## Reproduction

Amiga Arthur (`stories/Arthur - The Quest for Excalibur.adf`, release 54 serial
890606). Reach the churchyard, press F5, leave F5, then type `look`. Capture
`/dump-windows` at each step — the two already in the log are the before and the
during.

Related: SQ-1009 (which surfaced it and cleared itself), and CLAUDE.md's note that
v6 text is paint and survives `move_window`/`window_size`.
