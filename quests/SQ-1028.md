Hybrid lane (3b52f935, branch worktree-agent-a3bf5ce7afc777b60). Two findings redirect the quest.

1. The word the capture rules under (`Erasmus`, "This is the bridge of the Erasmus") is PROSE, and hybrid draws prose through `transcript::draw_str_runs` -> `render::apply_text_style`, NOT through `screen::v6_run_style`. Measured on `James Clavell's Shogun.adf` r295/s890321, two inputs past the boot menu: no PxText on that frame carries the Italic bit; the transcript style chunk does (7 chars, bits 4).

2. Per the user's rule: emphasis is ITALIC where a real italic FACE is available, UNDERLINE where none is, and we never synthesise a slope. On the cell paths the face is the player's terminal font and Modifier::ITALIC is the request for it, so hybrid is already correct and this lane changed no behaviour. The half the rule bites on is `render::bitfont` (lanthorn holds a release's own bitmap face, and no shipped face carries an italic variant) — a different axis from the per-machine `zvm::interpreter::V6Emphasis` table 5a465951 keyed on. Remaining work: ask TextFace "does this face have italics" rather than "does this machine underline", drop the synthesised shear, retire/retarget V6Emphasis. Needs the native_font.rs lane.

Shipped here: `render::TextInk` (honor + colours as one Copy value) across v6_run_style, its five draw functions and draw_str_runs — 14 production and 31 test call sites — plus `v6_shogun_emphasis_rule`, pinning the frame and the decision-neutral half of the rule (the bit arrives, as exactly one rendering, on the word and not the line) on both presses in both honor modes.
