  Measured while implementing saga_us.rs against the fifteen §12.13 specimens: command opcode 90 occurs in the binary action tables of six of the eight titles checked (11 times in the C64 Hulk, 3 in Pirate Adventure, 3 in Strange Odyssey, 2 in Adventureland, 2 in Apple II Mission Impossible) and NEVER in any of their .dat twins — it is exactly the "one extra command, and sometimes one extra parameter condition" §12.8 says the graphic release carries. Opcode 89, which this crate implements as ScottFree's draw-picture, also occurs in the same tables, so 90 is not simply a renumbering of it. lib.rs's "Where this crate KNOWINGLY disagrees with ScottFree" note records that Spatterlight renumbered 89 to 90 in its own fork; this corpus suggests 90 is a real SAGA-graphics command in its own right. Nothing was changed: lanthorn draws no Scott Adams pictures, so vm.rs's `_ => {}` arm makes 90 a no-op and the behaviour is correct today. For the spec half to name in §12.11 or §12.8 before anyone implements SAGA picture drawing.
