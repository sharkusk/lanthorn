## What we have and what is missing

`zvm::dictionary` already gives `load`, `words`, `entries`, `lookup`, `tokenise`
and `key_len`, so we know every word a story knows and can encode a candidate the
way the game would. `zvm::location::object_tree_view` and `find_player_object`
give the live object tree.

**What is missing is the SYNTAX.** The dictionary is a flat word list with no
parts of speech — verbs, nouns, adjectives and prepositions are all mixed in one
table. It cannot tell you that `TAKE` is a verb, that `TAKE <noun> FROM <noun>` is
a legal line while `TAKE <noun> WITH <noun>` is not, or which preposition a verb
expects.

That information IS in the story file. Inform 6 emits a grammar table (the verb
entries and their grammar lines); ZIL games carry syntax tables in the same
spirit. `infodump` from the ztools suite dumps both, so the formats are
documented and this is a solved parsing problem, not a research one.

## Why it belongs in zvm

Pure parsing of the story image, no I/O policy, no host seam — the same shape as
`dictionary.rs` and `objects.rs` that already live there. It is also machine- and
host-neutral knowledge, which is the standing argument for putting things in zvm
rather than `app` (SQ-1013 moved the Version 6 cell and standard window on exactly
that reasoning), and it is API surface an embedder outside lanthorn would want —
see `docs/zvm-embedding-review.md`.

**Zero external dependencies**, as ever. Hand-rolled, like every other parser in
that crate.

## Watch for

* **Version spread.** Grammar table layout differs between Inform 6 and the ZIL
  era, and across Z-machine versions. Verify against the specification and against
  real stories rather than inferring the shape from one game — CLAUDE.md's rule
  about verifying spec constants from authoritative sources applies directly, and
  a table read wrongly produces confident nonsense rather than an error.
* **Dictionary truncation.** v3 keys are 6 Z-characters, v4+ are 9, so grammar
  entries reference truncated words and two English words can collide. `key_len`
  already reports which.
* **Non-vacuity.** `unit_tests/` holds freely redistributable stories (`advent`,
  `minizork`, the Glulx and Scott works) — pin the extraction against those so CI
  actually exercises it, rather than only against gitignored commercial media.

## What it unblocks

SQ-1041 (offer the game's vocabulary on a rejection), SQ-1042 (scope-aware
completion) and any future disambiguation help. All three can be built crudely
from the dictionary alone and properly only with the grammar. **This is the piece
to do first.**

## Scheduled: 0.4, raised to high as SQ-1041's enabler

Tagged `theme=assist`, `release=0.4`.

`high` here means blocking, not urgent: SQ-1041 cannot offer the story's own
vocabulary usefully without parts of speech, so a rejection today can only answer
"this word is unknown" and not "this story spells that `light`, and it is a verb".
Ship this first or SQ-1041 ships degraded and gets rewritten.

Sequencing within the cluster: SQ-1045 (voice) → SQ-1044 (search) and SQ-1042
(completion) in parallel → this → SQ-1041 → SQ-1043. The middle pair are
independent of the grammar table and of each other, so they are the natural
parallel lanes.
