--- note 2026-09-28T04:25:47Z ---

Found while showing the item records in an embedding host's inventory/room views (lanthorn 9a6a219f). May overlap the item-detection audit already under way; listed so nothing is missed.
1. Which container: ItemLocation::Room { room, direct } (crates/mapper/src/graph.rs ~33-40) only flags nested; a host can't say 'in the brown sack' or 'on the table'. Ask: record the direct parent (container or supporter) item key when nested.
2. A gone item keeps no room: ItemLocation::Vanished (~48) drops where it was last seen, so a host can only say where it was first seen. Ask: keep the last room (and turn) on Vanished.
3. No pick-up turn: last_seen_turn advances every turn an item is carried (note_item_carried, graph.rs ~1402, called each turn from session.rs ~4606), so 'carried since move N' is lost. Ask: record the turn it became carried.
4. Carried items that disappear stay Carried: note_items_absent (~1437) sweeps only direct room sightings, so something eaten or destroyed while held is never Vanished. Ask: sweep carried items against the player's inventory too.
5. Scenery follows the player: in Zork I r88 (open mailbox, take leaflet, take mailbox, north, east, open window, enter window, open sack, take bottle, west, take lamp, take sword, east, turn on lamp, up, take rope, down, drop bottle, look) 'white house', 'board', 'stairs', 'chimney', 'kitchen window' and 'boarded window' are recorded as nested (direct: false) in each room entered, e.g. 'white house' moves West of House -> North of House -> Behind House, 'stairs' Kitchen -> Living Room -> Attic. They're room-wide scenery (local-globals), not portable things. Ask: leave them out (or mark them scenery) so they don't read as items that moved.
Correct at this pin: the mailbox marked fixed after a failed take; the leaflet nested -> carried; the dropped bottle's direct sighting.
