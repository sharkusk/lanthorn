Specimen: 'Zork Zero - The Revenge of Megaboz.adf' (Amiga), the DEFINE command's Function Keys screen, in Hybrid.

The terminal routes this frame to its cell path, not the ring. The route is has_menu && hybrid && !menu_over_art (render/screen.rs:757-830 at d0f4f682; the route label is at :2047, 'painted menu takeover routed here'). The cell path's base comes from the story window's own page via status_style (screen.rs:1878-1887, whose comment names this exact screen). On the Amiga that's Standard(10), #adadad. So the terminal draws the title and labels black on grey, and the definitions and selection #adadad on black.

hybrid_chrome_layout (screen.rs:5925) still returns Some for this frame, with the whole menu as story_overlay, resolved against the ring's host pair (#ffffff on the machine's #424242). Its doc promises None wherever the terminal draws no ring. A host that follows it draws dark text (#424242) on black and dark bars. PC r393 and Mac come out in the right colours only because their pages happen to coincide.

Wanted, any of:
(a) hybrid_chrome_layout returns None for a painted-menu takeover, and the route is published: a predicate such as hybrid_painted_menu_takeover(items, cell) -> bool, or a reason field on the plan;
(b) the cell path's layout for such a frame: base ink and ground (status_style), erase fills (draw_erase_fills ~6670), and runs as draw_painted_screen (~6699) places them, including story_shift and the anchored band (~1925-2090);
(c) at least, per story_overlay run (or once per layout), the resolved ink and ground the terminal actually stamps.

Test: the Amiga DEFINE screen, where each published cell's colours equal the terminal's cell render (the title and labels #000000 on #adadad; the definitions #adadad on #000000).
