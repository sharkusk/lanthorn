## What was seen

On the Macintosh **colour** press, with `set-v6-pixel-lock` enabled, more of each
descender disappears than without it. Reported by eye against the 7x15 cell
SQ-0917 introduced.

## Why this is probably NOT the same bug as the face fallback

Two descender defects were in play the same afternoon and they must not be
conflated:

1. **The face fallback** (fixed in SQ-0917's follow-up): `blit_glyph_styled` chose
   between the 8x16 face and the 8x8 master on `ch >= 16`, so a 15-tall cell fell
   through to the 8x8 chain. Measured at 15 tall, `y` bottoms 3 rows below `x`
   with the proper face and only **1** with the fallback — the tail is
   substantially there in both, just far shallower in one.
2. **This**, which is an ADDITIONAL loss on top, and only with pixel lock, and
   only reported on the colour press.

The blit happens at the NATIVE cell and knows nothing about pixel lock, so a
defect that only appears when the lock is on is almost certainly downstream of the
glyph — in how the native canvas is then scaled to the pane. A descender is the
bottom one or two scanlines of a glyph, which is exactly what a resample drops
first when it lands on an unlucky ratio.

## Where to look

- `v6_pixel_lock` and the locked scale path (SQ-0940, SQ-0978). The lock changes
  the scale to an integer ratio; work out what ratio the Mac COLOUR press lands on
  and whether the bottom rows of each 15-tall cell survive it.
- The colour press is 640x400 and the mono 480x300, so they lock to different
  ratios against the same pane — which would explain why only one was reported.
  **Check the mono press too before assuming it is colour-only.**
- Compare against the same frame with the lock off, at the same pane size, so the
  only variable is the lock.

## Do not start until

SQ-0917's render path is actually complete. As of filing, `render/screen.rs` still
carries **35 production sites** that hardcode 8 and 16 as bare literals rather
than reading the session cell, so Macintosh geometry is half-applied and any
measurement taken now is of a screen with two disagreeing cell sizes in it. That
work has to land first or this quest will chase a symptom of it.
