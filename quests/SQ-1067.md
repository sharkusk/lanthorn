--- note 2026-08-24T22:14:52Z ---

THE COMMENT. `crates/blorb/src/lib.rs:569-572`, on `sibling_blorb_by_name`:

    /// **Same match order and rule as [`resolve_resource_blorb`]:** an exact
    /// same-stem `.blb`/`.blorb`/`.zblorb` sibling first, else the best
    /// unambiguous [`stem_prefix_match`] among the directory's resource blorbs

Mirrored on the consumer side at `crates/app/src/picker.rs:1655`: "…or (like the info panel's resource resolution) an unambiguous stem-prefix match…". The ORDER is the same. The extension sets are not — and they differ in BOTH steps, in OPPOSITE directions, so neither function is a superset of the other.

BOTH SIDES.

  step 1, exact same-stem
    crates/blorb/src/lib.rs:520   resolve_resource_blorb   for ext in ["blb", "blorb"]
    crates/blorb/src/lib.rs:579   sibling_blorb_by_name    const EXTS: [&str; 3] = ["blb", "blorb", "zblorb"]

  step 2, directory scan
    crates/blorb/src/lib.rs:544-546  resolve  e == "blb" || e == "blorb" || e == "zblorb" || e == "gblorb"
    crates/blorb/src/lib.rs:579      sibling  the same EXTS — no "gblorb"

The doc sentence describes neither: it gives `resolve`'s step 1 a `.zblorb` it does not have.

OBSERVABLE DEFECT. Measured by the audit lane against the real crate with synthetic resource-only (no `Exec`) sidecars:

    story=game.ulx       sidecar=game.gblorb                resolve=true   tag=false
    story=gamestory.ulx  sidecar=game.gblorb                resolve=true   tag=false
    story=game.z5        sidecar=game.zblorb, game2.zblorb  resolve=false  tag=true
    story=game.z5        sidecar=game.blb,    game2.blb     resolve=true   tag=true   <- the .blb control

So two opposite lies in the story list:
  * a Glulx story with a resource-only `.gblorb` sidecar LOADS its resources and the row never lights `(blorb)` — the picker's step 2 cannot see `gblorb` at all;
  * a same-stem `.zblorb` caught in a prefix TIE lights `(blorb)` while resolution returns nothing — because `sibling`'s step 1 catches it before the tie is reached and `resolve`'s step 1 does not. The identical shape spelled `.blb` resolves, which is what makes the `.blb` row the control.

The tag is consumed at `crates/app/src/picker.rs:1659` -> `compute_row_badges`, so this is a user-visible claim about a file, made by the row, contradicted by what the launch actually does.

NOT TESTED. The six `sibling_by_name_*` cases in `crates/blorb/src/lib.rs` exercise `.blb` and `.blorb` only — every extension where the two functions happen to agree. `cargo nextest run -p blorb` is 292/292 green with the divergence in place.

CONFIDENCE. High on the divergence: it is four literal lists read side by side and reproduced empirically. LOW on real-corpus frequency — a resource-only `.gblorb` is unusual (a `.gblorb` normally carries the `Exec` chunk and IS the story), and the `.zblorb` case needs a prefix tie. No case was found on the user's own shelves; the evidence is synthetic.

SUGGESTED FIX (not applied — audit lane). One extension list, `pub(crate) const RESOURCE_BLORB_EXTS`, consulted by both steps of both functions — the cure the refactoring policy prescribes for a hand-maintained list. Whether `resolve`'s step 1 should GAIN `zblorb`/`gblorb` or `sibling`'s step 1 should LOSE `zblorb` is a real decision, not a mechanical one: step 1 is "the sidecar is named for this exact story", and a `.gblorb` so named is usually the story itself, which is why `resolve` reads the file and `sibling` cannot. Whichever way it goes, add a case covering `.zblorb` and `.gblorb` — every existing one is blind to this.
