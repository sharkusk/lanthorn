## Refiled 2026-08-27 — the original framing was wrong

This was filed as "let the player browse what verbs a story understands", as
though it needed a new surface. **The surface already exists**: the command band
(**F2**, `/open-command-band`) has a VERB column, documented in
`docs/features/interface.md`. The user caught it.

What is actually wrong is the column's **source**, and that is a better quest.

## The duplication, precisely

**1. `BUILTIN_VERBS` — a hardcoded 36-verb table** in
`crates/app/src/render/command_band.rs:118`, surfaced by `default_verbs()` and
wired in from `layout.rs:318`, `pane_drag.rs:238`, `input.rs:7278` and
`config.rs:657`. Nothing feeds it the running story's grammar — the only
`grammar` mentions in that file are doc comments about column shapes.

So the band shows a **generic IF verb set**, not this story's. It offers verbs a
game does not have and omits ones it does, and a player who trusts it is misled
by the one panel whose whole job is to say what is possible.

**2. `Arity` + `VerbEntry.prep` — the grammar model, re-derived by hand.**
`command_band.rs:73` defines `Solo` / `Object` / `ObjectOpt` / `Pair` with a
separate `prep: Option<String>`. That is exactly what `grammar_model::SyntaxLine`
already expresses — `noun_count()` plus literal preposition slots — and
`SyntaxLine` expresses it *better*, because a real verb has SEVERAL lines
(`TAKE noun` and `TAKE noun FROM noun` are two), where `Arity` forces one shape
per verb and cannot represent the alternation.

The band's own documentation gives the game away: *"Each verb declares its
shape, so only the columns that can come next are offered."* That is the grammar
table's structure, rebuilt as a constant, three years before the reader existed.

**3. `DEFAULT_QUICK`** (`command_band.rs:164`) is the direction/quick-action row.
Probably fine to leave — it is a UI convenience row, not a claim about the story
— but check whether the directions could equally come from the grammar, and say
what you decided.

## What is NOT duplicated (checked, do not "fix" these)

- `crates/app/src/vocab.rs` (SQ-1041) carries no verb list; its only constants
  are `MIN_LEN` and `ARTICLES`, and its `"examine"` is inside a test.
- `crates/verb-synonyms` ships harvested spellings for a different purpose —
  bridging a REJECTED word to a known one. That is not the band's job and the
  two should not be merged.
- `inventory.rs` reads `short_name` for the object columns. Different concern,
  and see the caveat below.

## The design question this raises, which needs deciding not defaulting

`[command_band] verbs` currently **replaces** the table wholesale and
`extra_verbs` extends it (`config.rs:636`). If the grammar drives the column,
what do those keys mean? Options: they still override entirely; they extend what
the grammar found; or `verbs` becomes a filter/ordering hint. Existing configs
must keep working — decide deliberately and document it.

Also: all three engines must answer. `zvm::grammar` and `gvm::grammar` give
verbs with syntax lines; `scott::Database` gives `verbs`/`nouns` with a fixed
`VERB NOUN` shape. A story whose grammar cannot be read (SQ-1101's non-Inform
files, a Glulx image whose chain will not close) must fall back to
`BUILTIN_VERBS` rather than showing an empty column — so the constant stays, as
a **fallback**, and stops being the primary source.

## Caveat worth carrying

The object columns append a word guessed from the object's **short name** (what
is printed) rather than its **parse-name property** (what can be typed) — see
SQ-1042. That is a separate defect, not this quest, but it is the same class of
bug: a panel telling the player something the parser has not confirmed.
