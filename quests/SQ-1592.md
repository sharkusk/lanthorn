--- note 2026-09-26T04:14:02Z ---

A host that draws chrome runs itself (text in its own fonts; line characters at a fixed stroke width) must treat text and lines drawn over a picture as part of the picture (they scale with it), and must know which space runs with a background are reverse bars rather than line furniture. Both answers exist privately: ChromeRowOracle::over_art (render/screen.rs ~8666) and row_is_reverse_bar (render/screen.rs:7418, pub(crate)). Wanted: expose them per run on V6TextRun (e.g. over_art: bool, bar: bool) or through a public query, so hosts don't restate the rules.
