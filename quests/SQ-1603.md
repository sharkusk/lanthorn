--- note 2026-09-26T17:21:39Z ---

set_glk_cell_px and TerminalFacts::glk_cell_px take whole pixels. A host with fractional cells has to round them, so a 28-cell rule canvas comes out 280px in a 285.6px window. Wanted: accept an f32 (or a rational) cell and compute each window's canvas size from its exact extent, rounding once per window rather than once per cell.
