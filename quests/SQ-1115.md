## Decision: no taste filter. Faithful to the corpus. (user, 2026-08-27)

The lane flagged that the corpus's profanity comes through from games that
implement it (`fuck shit piss damn curse`), and that the pre-existing WordNet
half already ships `rape molest ravish` from a synset. It declined to add a
blocklist and asked for a ruling.

**The ruling is faithful — no filter, no blocklist, not now and not later.**
"We don't censor in this house."

Recorded here so it is not re-litigated by a future lane that stumbles on the
same rows and assumes nobody noticed. They were noticed, escalated, and this is
the answer.

**And the invariant makes it defensible rather than merely permitted.** No word
in this table is ever *shown* unless the story being played has it in its own
dictionary — SQ-1041's `offer` unconditionally `retain`s candidates the
dictionary holds, and that filter sits in one place precisely so no source can
bypass it. So a suggestion is always the game's own vocabulary reflected back,
never lanthorn's invention. A game that implements `damn` is a game whose author
put `damn` in it, and a player who reaches for it is entitled to be told the
parser will accept it.

The lane's own reasoning for refusing was right and is worth preserving: a
blocklist is exactly the "list of words someone decided were bad" that a
generated, auditable, regenerable table exists to avoid. The register filter it
does apply is a **frequency** judgement made by Beale's corpus — an external,
citable source — not a taste judgement made by us.
