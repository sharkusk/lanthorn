--- note 2026-09-26T04:14:03Z ---

On the Menu bottom plan, fill_menu_flank_extension (render/screen.rs:7733) paints the flank rules it carries down through the extension as art pixels, even under RecordOnly. The borders above the extension are recorded as runs, so a host that draws runs itself (e.g. line characters at a fixed stroke width rather than scaled) sees the carried-down segments scale with the art while the borders above don't: a visible jog where they meet. Seen on Journey PC r83 and Amiga on panes tall enough for the Menu plan. Wanted: under RecordOnly, record the carried-down flank rules as runs (same font/cells as the borders they continue) instead of painting them.
