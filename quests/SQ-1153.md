## The evidence, from two lanes that did not know about each other

`zvm::grammar::decode_roles` reads `GrammarFormat::InfocomV6` using **Inform's** flag layout — `$01` verb, `$80` noun. Infocom's V6 titles do not keep them there.

**SQ-1135's measurement**, taken through real sessions rather than by reading bytes:

| story | what the noun bit selects |
|---|---|
| Arthur r74 | `are is was were will` — and **not** `crystal`, `torque`, `sword` |
| Zork Zero r393 | `a all and of the then` |
| Shogun r322 | nothing at all |

Every one of those is wrong, and wrong in a different way, which is what makes the layout unguessable from one title. Arthur's own bytes fit `$01`/`$02`/`$04`/`$40` neatly — and Zork Zero and Shogun both falsify that reading, so a lane that had only looked at Arthur would have shipped a confident wrong answer.

**SQ-1120's independent finding**, from the adjectives work: `decode_roles` does not decode `adjective` for the V6 layout **at all**. That lane also measured the DESC bit on the three V6 titles — Zork Zero 11 of 1624 words, Shogun 17 of 1389, Arthur 9 of 1059 — against a fifth to a third of the dictionary on every pre-V6 Infocom story. Those numbers are not a sparse adjective list; they are a bit that means something else here.

Two lanes arriving at the same conclusion from opposite directions is the strongest evidence in the quest.

## Why it has not bitten harder

Nothing user-facing depended on V6 roles until now, and both lanes routed around rather than through:

* SQ-1120 gated its adjective detection on **version >= 4** and reads a property rather than the flag byte.
* SQ-1135 added `Introspect::all_object_words` and asks the OBJECTS which words file under them, instead of asking the dictionary what kind of word it is. Glulx and Scott keep the role-bit filter; only zvm takes the new route.

So this is currently a wrong answer nobody is asking. That will not stay true — `WordRoles` is public API on a crate whose stated priority is to be embeddable outside lanthorn, and an embedder has no way to know the V6 answers are fiction.

## What solving it needs

**Do not guess from one title.** That is the trap this quest exists to record: Arthur alone supports a clean, wrong hypothesis. Any candidate layout must be checked against all three V6 titles in `stories/` — `arthur-r74-s890714.z6`, `zork0-r393-s890714.z6`, `shogun-r322-s890706.z6` — and predict all three.

**The parser is the oracle**, as SQ-1118 and SQ-1120 both established: a proposed noun bit is right when `zvm-cli` accepts the words it selects in a noun position and rejects the ones it does not. A reader verified only against itself proves nothing, and this is precisely the defect class that produced the wrong answer in the first place.

**A useful cross-check already exists**: SQ-1135's `all_object_words` gives, per V6 title, the set of words the game itself files under objects. A correct noun bit should agree with that set closely. Disagreement in either direction is diagnostic rather than fatal — Arthur's `password` object rewrites its own parse names mid-puzzle — but it is a far stronger signal than counting words.

**Check whether V6 is even one layout.** Three titles from one publisher across 1988–89 is a small sample, and Zork Zero selecting function words while Shogun selects nothing may mean two different things rather than one bug.

Related: [[SQ-1120]] shipped the V4+ adjectives and holds the DESC census; [[SQ-1135]] holds the noun-bit measurement and the `all_object_words` seam that routes around it.
