  Surfaced while implementing SQ-1414's C64 Mysterious loader: stories/scott-dialects/c64/QUESTPR1.D64 carries SHULK.DB at $8030 (uncrunched, signature at $8308), a second zero-pass C64 case the spec's §7.2 subset covers — but it is NOT the §4-§6 memory-image format at all. Spec §11 ("The UK Hulk releases are not this format") states what it is instead: a fifteen-word header in the US field order, a dictionary found by scanning for the literal bytes ANY, LENGTH-PREFIXED strings (a length byte of 0 meaning the string "."), item locations stored two bytes apart, a per-item four-byte picture lookup, an action table stored COLUMN-major, room connections direction-major, and a baseline of the dictionary hit minus the catalogued address minus 645. Nothing in §§4-6 applies, so it needs a separate dialect module rather than a branch in crates/scott/src/c64.rs; §11 says to refuse or implement it separately, and SQ-1414 refuses it. Its pictures are spec §8.3 family C (four-colour strip bitmaps), also out of scope for the family-B decoder. QUESTPR3.D64 (Fantastic Four) is unidentified and may be a third case.
