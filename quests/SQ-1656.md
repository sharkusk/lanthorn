  User reported two symptoms that are the same root mechanism: (1) during CM's accessibility Q&A, after answering the first question it "scrolls off the top, despite having lots of room on the story screen"; (2) "before the screen has filled" in both Anchorhead and CM, taking an action makes the story briefly scroll backward then snap forward. Both reproduce the pre-existing window_wrapped_rows top-anchor rule (crates/app/src/render/transcript.rs:1201): whenever content since clear_anchor still fits the viewport, the view pins it to the top and hides everything before the anchor, regardless of why the anchor moved. SQ-1654 (the scrollback-preservation fix) made mark_screen_clear() run on clears that now PRESERVE old content instead of destroying it, but the top-anchor logic still treats every anchor identically — so content that now legitimately survives in scrollback gets hidden above the fold anyway, exactly as if it had been destroyed. Not a bug in SQ-1654's collapse-vs-preserve decision itself; a pre-existing render assumption ("nothing meaningful precedes a clear anchor") that the preservation fix breaks.
