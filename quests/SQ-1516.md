--- note 2026-09-15T00:04:00Z ---

Reproduced on zvm-cli TTY: v3 status is pinned at row 1 (DECSTBM 2;24 + CUP 1;1) after the game has already streamed into row 1. Lurking Horror loses "You've waited until the last minute again..."; Zork I would lose "West of House". screen.rs explicitly skips the 1-row erase-shift (erase_then_single_row_status_does_not_shift) to avoid scrolling a full page off the bottom. --story-only / non-TTY keep the line. gvm-cli confines the buffer to its Glk rect from the start; scott-cli has no overlay status. Fix direction: reserve the status row (enter_region) before any story print, rather than overlaying after.
