## Reported behaviour, verbatim enough to reproduce

`stories/anchor.z8`, the in-game help/menu screen.

**In lanthorn:** arrowing down loses the last three lines of the screen at once —
and with them the `BACKSPACE to return to game` line that sits below the menu.
The cursor can still be moved onto those bottom three rows and can still select
from them, so the entries are live; only their painted text is gone.

**In `zvm-cli`:** the same three lines are erased, but one at a time — each goes
as the cursor moves over it, rather than all three together.

**In both:** entering a help SUB-MENU and coming back leaves the lines intact.
They are not erased again.

## Why this is filed as engine-side

It reproduces under `zvm-cli`, which shares `zvm` with the app but none of the
app's rendering, wrap cache or pane layout. That is the same control that
separated the two halves in SQ-1084 — where the app was at fault and zvm-cli was
correct. Here they BOTH misbehave, which points at `zvm` (window/erase handling
around the menu's cursor moves) rather than at the TUI.

The difference in HOW they fail — three at once against one at a time — is worth
keeping rather than dismissing as cosmetic: it suggests the app is repainting a
region the engine has already blanked, while zvm-cli shows the blanking as it
happens. Whatever the erase is, both are downstream of it.

And the fact that it stops after a sub-menu round trip says the state is
recoverable — something about the FIRST paint of that screen differs from the
repaint, which is usually where a split/erase-window/cursor assumption is
hiding.

## Where to start

Reproduce under `zvm-cli` first, since it is the smaller system, and capture the
opcode sequence around the arrow-key moves — `erase_window`, `erase_line`,
`split_window` and `set_cursor` near the bottom rows of the upper window. Compare
the first paint against the post-sub-menu repaint; the difference between those
two is the bug.
