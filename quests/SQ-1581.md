--- note 2026-09-25T20:05:01Z ---

Current code (rev d7fd62b7): `draw_secondary_prose_into` (crates/app/src/render/v6_layout.rs ~l.2133-2137) walks each line's chars and `break`s when `pen + adv > right`. The rest of the line is dropped, not wrapped.

Evidence: FMV Poker (`fmvpoker.z6` + `.blb`).
- After a draw, the panel reads "You draw (a) a Four, … and (e) a Thre" and loses "e.".
- On a narrow pane, "…a Six.  Us" is cut.
- The lib's own Rasterise composite shows the same, so the TUI does too.

Change: wrap panel prose at word boundaries within the window's width, as the story window's prose wraps (the game printed it as flowing text). Fall back to a character break only for an unbreakable word longer than the window.

Acceptance: FMV Poker's draw message shows in full across two lines at 640×400 and on a narrow pane. Other panel users (Zork Zero, Arthur) are unchanged where their text already fits.
