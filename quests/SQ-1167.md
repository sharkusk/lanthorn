## What to show

The launch-options dialog (`crates/app/src/launch_options.rs`, SQ-0789/0791) — the
boot-time choices a story can only be STARTED with. Nothing in the gallery's
twenty-six frames shows it, and it is one of the picker's three doors.

The user asked for it **"with a game that has a bunch of options"**, which decides
the subject. The panel admits exactly two things, and only one of them varies by
story: the PICTURE ARCHIVE. So the richest story is the one with the most native
archives beside it.

Counted in `stories/`:

| story | archive candidates |
|---|---|
| **Arthur** | **6** — `Arthur.blb`, `arthur.cg1`, `arthur.eg1`, `arthur.eg2`, `arthur.mg1`, `arthur.pic` |
| Zork Zero | 5 |
| Journey | 5 |
| Shogun | 4 |

**Arthur is the pick.** Six renditions plus the interpreter-number row is a panel
with something to say; a story with one archive would show an empty-looking dialog
and teach nothing.

## Reaching it

Three doors, one constructor (SQ-0789): **Shift-Enter**, **`o`**, and a double
RIGHT-click, all routing to `BrowserAction::OpenLaunchOptions` →
`open_launch_options`. There is also the slash command `open-launch-options`
(`Context::Browser`). For a capture recipe `o` on a selected row is the simplest —
but drive it against the real picker rather than assuming, exactly as SQ-1164 had to
for Journey.

`story-list` is the pattern to follow for a picker-based shot: `media =
"frontispieces"` (a directory rather than a file), a `wait` long enough for the
library to settle, then keys to select a row.

## The guard — and this is the third time today

`expect` on this shot must name something that is only on the DIALOG, not on the
picker behind it. Two gallery shots were found today passing on the wrong screen —
five Journey frames stopping at a menu, and `macintosh-embedded-face` captured on a
`[more]` pager — both because `expect` named something visible either way.

So: assert on the archive names the panel lists, or the interpreter-number row, or
the dialog's own title. Something that CANNOT be on screen unless the dialog opened.
SQ-1164's `expect_prose_cells` is the wrong tool here (a dialog is not prose), so
this needs its own honest assertion rather than a borrowed one.

## Worth stating in the caption

The panel's admission rule is a genuinely good piece of design and the caption is a
place to say it: **an option is admitted only if it cannot be changed after boot.**
The picture archive qualifies because its pictures resolve as the story starts; the
interpreter number qualifies because header `$1E` is read by the STORY. v6 render
mode was checked and REJECTED — `/set-v6-render` switches live, so it belongs in the
settings screen, and duplicating it here would create two editors for one value.
That is the same contract SQ-1161 just settled for the settings screen, seen from
the other side.

## Do not

Delete any existing shot — curation is an open editorial question the user has not
settled. And note SQ-1165 and SQ-1166 also touch `gallery.toml`; these three want
sequencing rather than parallelism.
