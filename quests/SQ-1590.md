--- note 2026-09-26T03:42:35Z ---

`parse_inventory_output` (crates/app/src/inventory.rs:124) turns an `inventory` command's output into held items by treating every non-blank line after a "carrying"/"holding"/"have"/"you are empty" header as one more item. It can't tell an item line from the story's own narration printed on the same turn, and it doesn't split a single-sentence header ("You are carrying a pile of napkins.") into its item.

Repro, headless through the library (zork0-r393-s890714.z6 from stories/): boot, then `inventory`, `northeast` (Banquet Hall to Scullery), `inventory`. The second turn prints:
  You are carrying a pile of napkins.
     "When I give an order, servant, I mean NOW!" The force of the voice is almost enough to propel you southward.
`state.inventory_fallback` (host/turn.rs:742, set whenever the command is i/inv/inventory) becomes exactly:
  ["\"When I give an order, servant, I mean NOW!\" The force of the voice is almost enough to propel you southward."]
The narration is kept as an item, and "pile of napkins" is lost.

Story-independent:
  parse_inventory_output("You are carrying a pile of napkins.\n   \"When I give an order, servant, I mean NOW!\" The force of the voice is almost enough to propel you southward.")
returns the narration line as an item.

Where it surfaces: inventory_fallback is the carried-items list whenever the player object is unresolved (render/command_band.rs:930/933 refresh_objects feeding the command band's carried and second-object columns; render/inventory_dock.rs and render/transcript.rs:1719, the inventory panel). Any host showing those columns or that panel shows the narration as a held object.

Suggested direction: parse the header sentence's own list ("a pile of napkins", "an apple, a sword and a lamp"), stop at the end of the listing instead of consuming the rest of the turn, and never keep a line that reads as a full sentence (quotes, closing punctuation, sentence length) as an item.
