Direct evidence from my own headless probe (real `boot_story`/`finish_command_turn` path, CounterfeitMonkey-10.gblorb, fresh scratch home dir, answering "yes" to each Line prompt in sequence):

- Prompt: "Can you hear me? >>"
- Submit "yes" (turn 1): `result.transcript` == exactly `">"` — nothing else. State's transcript tail after this turn: [..., "Can you hear me? >> yes", ">"]. The expected reply text is NOT here.
- Submit "yes" again (turn 2): `result.transcript` == `"yes\nRight, we're Alexandra now. Before the synthesis, I was Alex. You were...\n\n>"` — the REAL reply to the FIRST "yes" shows up now, one full turn late, mixed in with the echo of the second "yes".

So CM's own printed reply to answering "Can you hear me?" is being delayed by exactly one turn at the engine level — captured by `GlulxSession` only on the NEXT `submit()` call rather than the one it was printed in response to. This smells like a Glk turn-boundary bug: CM likely does something unusual in how it resolves this specific prompt (possibly multiple glk_select cycles, or a deferred print) that our single-drain-per-submit model doesn't fully capture before returning control.

CORRECTION from the user, who is actually replaying this live in the real app: "Right, we're Alexandra now..." actually comes after answering the SECOND prompt, not as a delayed reply to the first. "We are completely missing a line in the story."

This means my earlier framing ("the reply is delayed by one turn, captured on the next submit") was WRONG about WHICH turn "Right, we're Alexandra now..." belongs to. Corrected understanding of CM's real intro structure:

1. Prompt: "Can you hear me? >>"
2. Player answers "yes"
3. CM SHOULD print some line of story text here (the lead-in to the second question) — THIS is the line that's genuinely missing, dropped, never recovered. In my probe, this turn's `TurnResult.transcript` came back as just `">"`.
4. The second prompt appears (as a bare, textless `>`, because its own lead-in text was dropped in step 3).
5. Player answers "yes" again.
6. CM prints "Right, we're Alexandra now..." — this is the CORRECT reply to the SECOND prompt, not a delayed reply to the first. It was never missing or delayed; it was always going to appear here.

So the actual bug is: whatever line of story text is meant to print between step 2 and step 4 is being dropped entirely by the engine (not delayed, not buffered-till-next-turn — genuinely lost), leaving the second prompt bare. The already-dispatched fix lane (agent a4af919125f788615) was briefed with the INCORRECT "delayed by one turn" framing — sending a correction via SendMessage so it looks for a genuine drop at that specific turn boundary rather than a buffering/off-by-one capture bug.
