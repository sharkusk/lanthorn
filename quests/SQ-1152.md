## What this shot is for

`V6RenderMode::Extended` (SQ-1032) is shipped and nothing in the gallery shows it. Its own doc states the claim to demonstrate:

> `Raster`, but the frame GROWS downward instead of letterboxing: the magnification is pinned to a whole number of device pixels per native pixel and the pane's surplus height buys whole text rows of prose in the game's own bitmap face. The game is told nothing — its own screen is the top of a taller canvas, laid out exactly as it always was.

A tall pane is the only place that is visible, which is why this is a tall shot.

## Why Shogun, and not another Zork Zero

**The gallery has no Shogun shot at any press** — checked, zero matches in `gallery.toml` — while Zork Zero already carries several. The corpus is well supplied for it: `James Clavell's Shogun.adf` (Amiga), `shogun_s1..s5.dsk` (Apple II, five disks), `Shogun.toast` (Macintosh), `Shogun.po` (ProDOS), `shogun-r322-s890706.z6` bare, plus `shogun.cg1` / `eg1` / `mg1` and `Shogun.blb`.

## Do NOT convert `zork0-mac-tall` to do this

That was the original idea and it is wrong. The comment block above that shot explains why it exists: it is *"the one press in this file that can PROVE"* hybrid tiles a border honestly. Every other rendition draws a featureless shaft, and on a featureless shaft "a stretch, a shift and an honest tile all look identical". `Pic.data` bands its pillar, so a reader counts rings — one at 32 rows in `zork0-mac-mono`, three at 58 in `zork0-mac-tall`, spaced as drawn. Converting it deletes that argument from the gallery.

The two are complements and read better side by side: **hybrid extends the BORDER; extended grows the FRAME.**

## Three things to settle, none of them hard

**1. The manifest has no `extended`.** `Shot::raster` is a `bool` and the writer at `pty_stream/gallery.rs:875` does `if shot.raster { "v6_render = \"raster\"\n" }`. A third mode needs a real field. Prefer a mode token over a second bool — two bools can express "raster and extended at once", which is not a state. Note the manifest already REFUSES `raster` on a library shot (SQ-1080: "a field that would be quietly ignored"), so whatever replaces it must keep that validation, and `gallery_manifest` will want a case for the new spelling.

**2. Raster guards differ, and the existing shots say how.** In raster the whole screen is one image, so there are no text cells for `expect` to search. Both `arthur-amiga-proportional` and the advent raster shot fall back to the **pane title** plus `expect_art_cells`, each with a comment saying why. A tall extended Shogun wants the same treatment — and `expect_art_cells` must be re-measured for this frame, set where a frame that FAILED to extend cannot reach it, exactly as `zork0-mac-tall`'s 1200 is reasoned.

**3. Which press.** The Macintosh MONOCHROME press is the structurally safest choice for a pixel-locked mode: it is `art_scale (1, 1)`, so a whole ART rung is a whole NATIVE rung and it cannot hit the half-rung problem that makes the Macintosh COLOUR press's text look wrong (SQ-1012, SQ-1024). **But verify Shogun's own numbers rather than inheriting Zork Zero's** — Shogun differs from the rest of the corpus in ways this repo has already logged (its border slab is wider than every other title's flank), so measure its picture space and scale before committing to a press. `machine-screenshots/amiga-shogun-game.png` exists if a real-machine cross-check is wanted.

## Sequencing

**After SQ-1138 lands.** That lane is repairing the word reveal under v6 raster and may be working in `pty_stream` itself, so the two would collide — and more to the point, its fix could change what an extended frame contains. Pinning `expect_art_cells` against a mode mid-repair risks baking in a number that is about to move.

## Verification

The gallery's own rule: a shot is a fixture. Name the story file, the release, the press and the turn count, and give the shot a guard that a frame which failed to do the thing cannot satisfy — which for this one means an art-cell floor that an unextended, letterboxed frame falls short of.

## Answering the three things the quest left to settle

**1. The manifest field.** Done as asked — a mode token, not a second bool. `Shot::raster: bool` is now `Shot::v6_render: Option<app::config::V6RenderMode>`, and the config writer goes through `app::config::v6_render_key` rather than a literal, so the token the manifest accepts and the token the run's `config.toml` is parsed back with are one spelling (SQ-1079's reason, one layer out). `Option` rather than a defaulted enum is what keeps the SQ-1080 library refusal able to tell an author who asked for a mode from one who said nothing — and the refusal is on the FIELD, so an `extended` library shot is as dead as a `raster` one was. Unlike the app's deliberately forgiving config deserializer, a manifest typo is an error (`v6_render = "rastr"` is refused, not silently hybrid). New case `the_render_mode_is_a_token_the_app_itself_parses` covers all three tokens, the round-trip against `v6_render_key`, the typo, the retired `raster = true`, and that the committed manifest exercises both non-default modes; `a_library_shot_may_not_describe_a_story` gained the `extended` row. 28 cases in `gallery_manifest`, all green.

**2. The raster guards.** Followed `arthur-amiga-proportional` / `macintosh-embedded-face`: pane title in `expect` (`James Clavell's Shogun.adf`) plus a re-measured `expect_art_cells`. Measured on this frame: **3280 cells, which is 80x41 — the entire pane content.** A frame that DECLINED to extend letterboxes 640x400 at the same 2x into 1280x800 and covers 80x25 = **2000**. The floor is **2400**, between the two with room on both sides — so it is the extension itself, not the artwork, exactly as the quest required.

**3. Which press — and the reasoning in the quest is superseded, worth recording.** The Macintosh monochrome press was suggested for its `art_scale (1, 1)`. **Shogun has no Macintosh press in the corpus** (`Shogun.toast` is a Toast CD image and is in no media table; the presses are the Amiga .adf r295, the two Apple II r311 sets, and the bare r322 + `Shogun.blb`). It also turns out not to matter: `RasterFrame::extended` floors to a whole number of device pixels per **NATIVE** pixel, which is strictly stricter than `v6_pixel_lock`'s whole-device-per-ART rung, so the SQ-1012/SQ-1024 half-rung problem **cannot arise in extended mode on any press**. The Amiga floppy is therefore the right pick on its merits: it carries its own bitmap face as well as its own plates (SQ-1053), and the extension's whole subject is TEXT. `machine-screenshots/amiga-shogun-game.png` is the same press on a real machine to read it against.

**Consequence: no `--v6-pixel-lock on`, where both existing raster shots pass it.** The mode already pins a stricter magnification; passing the lock would be a second, weaker opinion about the same number. `size = "82x44"` still pins the rung — `(82-2)*16 / 640` is exactly 2 — and every row past the 28th is surplus the mode spends. At 44 rows that is 656 native rows: the game's own 400 plus **sixteen** whole 16-pixel text rows.

## `zork0-mac-tall` untouched, as instructed

It keeps its ring-counting argument. The two are placed as complements in the manifest's prose: **hybrid extends the BORDER, extended grows the FRAME.**

## One thing worth knowing for the next key spec

The committed spec is `wait:4000,cr,wait:2500,cr,wait:3000,text:look,cr,wait:4000` — splash, START, look — and the comment above it says why.

Two corrections from the user applied on the same branch (commit 68267389). Both are standing rules for EVERY shot, not for the Shogun row.

## 1. The pixel lock is on for the whole gallery

Was `--v6-pixel-lock on` in two shots' `args`, which read as a property of those two frames. It is a property of the set: `v6_pixel_lock = true` now goes into every run's config, the two per-shot copies are gone, and `--v6-pixel-lock` joined `--image-protocol` / `--user-dir` / `--sound` on the tool-owned list so no shot can turn it off.

**Verified by capture, not by argument.** The four presses that had never been locked come out identical: `journey-blorb` 2.000x, `zork0-mac-mono` 3.000x, `zork0-mac-tall` 3.000x (480x300 Macintosh press), `journey-halfblocks` 3.000x — every guard still met. That is what `every_v6_shot_magnifies_by_a_whole_number` already implied: it fails any shot whose FREE scale is fractional, and a whole number is always a valid rung, so on a correctly sized shot the lock has nothing to snap to. It is a floor under the sizes, not a change to them.

**My per-shot "why no lock" argument is withdrawn** — the technical content stands (extended floors to whole DEVICE pixels per NATIVE pixel, strictly finer than the per-ART rung, so SQ-1012/1024 cannot arise there) but it is now a sentence recording that the mode does not NEED the lock, not a reason to omit it. Consistency across the gallery beats a per-shot optimisation.

## 2. Every shot draws the patched-font icons

The frames are rasterised through a Nerd Font and were nonetheless drawing the plain Geometric Shapes fallback — `zork1-map` and `shogun-amiga-extended` both reported `NO GLYPH ANYWHERE FOR: ◈◌`.

The harness now calls the app's **own** `app::style::write_font_check_answer(path, true)` — literally what a `yes` to `/run-font-check` writes — into the run's `style.toml`, rather than spelling `nerdfont` in the harness. So the gallery cannot drift from the presets, and a later improvement to them reaches these frames for free. Re-captured: the warning is gone on both, and the map's arrows, portal stairs and pane-border controls are the patched glyphs.

**What that answer does not set, so the absence is not read as an oversight: the six `badge_*` glyphs.** They are `[elements]` keys with no `nerdfont` preset behind them and the font check has never touched them. Nerd codepoints in the picker's badges would be a decision about the APP's presets; freezing six literals in a capture harness is exactly what `write_font_check_answer`'s own doc argues against. **If the user wants nerd badges in the two picker shots, that wants a preset in the app first — worth its own quest.**

## Where both live, and why there

`write_run_settings(user_dir, shot, media)` — split out of `capture` so a test can read the written files back without a pty. Its doc says outright that this is where a setting the whole gallery should agree on goes, so the next shot added inherits both rather than remembering them. The seam already existed for `random_seed` and `default_story_dir`.

Guarded by `every_shot_captures_pixel_locked_and_with_the_patched_font_icons`, which reads the files back the way lanthorn reads them (`load_style` → `finalize_symbols`) instead of matching the strings the harness wrote, and pins the three preset names against `font_check_dialog`'s own constants. `a_shot_may_not_pass_an_argument_the_tool_owns` now also sweeps the committed manifest for all four owned arguments.
