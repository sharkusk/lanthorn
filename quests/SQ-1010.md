## Reproduced at the reported 85x38, under the pty

`stories/Arthur - The Quest for Excalibur.adf` (Amiga r54/890606), F2 map screen.
Route: one keypress, `n` to "Would you like to restore a saved position?", ten
more keypresses through the intro, then **F2**.

```
cargo run -q -p app --example pty_capture -- \
  --story "stories/Arthur - The Quest for Excalibur.adf" --size 85x38 \
  --keys 'wait:3000,cr,wait:1500,text:n,wait:1500,cr,wait:600,…(x10),f2,wait:2000' \
  --png map85.png
```

(The `n` is a single keypress with **no** carriage return, and it comes AFTER one
keypress, not before — sending it first, or following it with `cr`, leaves the
game re-prompting "Please press Y or N>" forever and the capture never reaches
the game.)

## What the capture shows against the machine

`machine-screenshots/amiga-arthur-map.png`: the scroll is self-contained inside
the upper panel — rounded ends, its two ornate end-caps, and **grey screen either
side of it** right out to the frame's left and right edges.

lanthorn at 85x38: the end-cap columns are painted hard against the extreme left
and right screen edges, from the top of the frame down past the panel, with
fragments of them showing beside and behind the score bar. That is the classifier
claiming the map picture as a SIDE COLUMN instead of leaving it to the story —
exactly what this quest describes.

Swept 80x30, 100x40, 132x60 and 160x70 as well: the end-caps run to the panel's
bottom and leave fragments by the score bar at every one, so the defect is not
confined to 85x38 — only its severity moves with the pane. The user reports some
sizes replicate it down the whole screen.

## Not the same defect as SQ-1076

Checked while confirming the SQ-1076 split-window fix, on a build carrying it.
The map still does this, so it is untouched by that change and wants its own
look: SQ-1076 is about windows 0/1 being reset to full width by
`@split_window`, and this is `v6_border`'s flank classifier reading the map's
end-caps as a border column. `flank_shape` is the instrument —
`--story "stories/Arthur - The Quest for Excalibur.adf" --keys f2` — and remember
its `--cols` default of 17 is an instrument default rather than the app's crop
(see SQ-1029's note).
