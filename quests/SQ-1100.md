## Explicitly last, and conditional (user, 2026-08-27)

> "let's get the other stuff done, then we can leave the 'stuck trigger' for last. we
> may or may not need it based on the other helpers and their effectiveness."

So this is **not** part of the assist release's critical path. Build SQ-1045, 1040,
1041, 1042, 1043 and 1044 first, play with them, and only then ask whether a player
still gets stuck in a way the ladder does not already catch. If completion, the
vocabulary offer and transcript search remove enough friction, this may never be
worth building — and that is a good outcome, not a dropped feature.

## What it would be

The signals are cheap and already held: no new room in N turns (the mapper knows), no
score change, repeated parser rejections, the same command entered twice. Nothing new
would need to be recorded.

The action is to offer the next rung of the help ladder described on SQ-1045 —
ultimately the hints, which is the rung the assists deliberately do not reach.

## The reason it is hard, and why it is last rather than merely later

**Being asked "want a hint?" is itself information.** It tells the player there is
something to find HERE, which they did not know a moment ago. So a stuck-detector is
the one rung where being helpful and being spoilery are the same action, and an eager
one is a spoiler engine wearing a helpful face.

Consequences if it is ever built:

- **Reactive, not proactive** is the strong default — offered when the player asks for
  help, not volunteered when we think they need it. Volunteering is the version that
  leaks.
- Conservative thresholds, and trivially dismissible.
- It must never fire differently depending on whether the current room HAS a puzzle,
  because a detector that is quiet in empty rooms and chatty in puzzle rooms is a
  puzzle detector no matter what it is called.

## What would justify building it

A real observation — from the user or from play — that the other assists leave someone
stuck with nothing to reach for. Not a design argument. If that observation does not
arrive, close this as discarded rather than building it on principle.
