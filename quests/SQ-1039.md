## The code

`crates/app/src/native_font.rs`, `declared_cell`:

    Some((f, FaceFit::Metric)) =>
        V6Cell::new(cell.w, u16::from(f.height).saturating_mul(art_scale.1 as u16))

and `render::bitfont::blit_metric_glyph` draws a Metric face at its own bitmap
size times the same art scale.

## Why it is right on the Amiga and wrong on the Macintosh

**The Amiga's text and art share a coordinate space**, so one number serves both.
Measured today, and it holds for both games that matter:

* Arthur's `char.data` is drawn in the 320x200 ART space — its own advance table
  averages 5.21 face px and `amiga-arthur-text.png` measures 4.70 art px/char,
  which agree at 1:1 and are out by 2x at 2:1. Face height 10 x art scale 2 = 20
  native. Correct.
* Shogun's topaz is drawn in the 640-wide HIRES space — measured a flat 4.00 art
  px/char for an 8-wide face, i.e. one text px is HALF an art px wide. But
  vertically it is still one text row per art row, so 8 x 2 = 16 native. Also
  correct, by luck: the horizontal disagreement never reaches `declared_cell`,
  which only scales the HEIGHT and leaves the width at the profile's.

**The Macintosh colour press breaks the assumption outright.** `CPic.data` is
320x200 with `art_scale` (2, 2) onto the 640x400 unit screen, and the machine
draws TEXT at one native pixel per text pixel while an ART pixel is two. So:

    Geneva 12, height 15,  art_scale.1 = 2   ->  declared 30   WRONG, should be 15

The monochrome press escapes it: `Pic.data` is 480x300 at (1, 1), so 15 x 1 = 15.

This is the same art-versus-text density split that made `set-v6-pixel-lock`'s
half rungs break Macintosh colour text (SQ-1012, SQ-1024) — a whole ART rung is a
HALF-NATIVE one there, and raster text is drawn at the native cell. CLAUDE.md
tabulates it under "Art density and text density are different facts, and on one
machine they disagree."

## The fix in one sentence

`declared_cell` and the pen need a **TEXT scale**, which is not always the art
scale: it is `art_scale` on the Amiga and `(1, 1)` on every Macintosh press
regardless of what the archive states.

Where that fact should live is the interesting part. `art_scale` is the ARCHIVE's
(SQ-0790) and the cell is the MACHINE's (SQ-0917, SQ-1013), so a text scale is
machine knowledge and belongs beside the cell in `zvm::interpreter`'s table rather
than being derived in `app` — the same argument that moved `v6_cell` and
`v6_std_window` there. `MachineBoot` is the natural carrier, since it already
resolves both.

## Why it is invisible today

Nothing admits a `FaceFit::Metric` face except Arthur's Amiga floppy, and the
Amiga is precisely the machine where the two scales coincide. **SQ-1037 admitting
Geneva off a System disk is what would expose it**, which is why this is filed now
rather than found later on a frame nobody can explain.

## Acceptance

A Macintosh COLOUR press booted with a Metric face must declare a 15-row cell, not
30, and must draw its glyphs at 1 native px per face px while its artwork stays at
2. `machine-screenshots/mac-zorkzero-hint.png` and `mac-zorkzero-game.png` are the
references — the latter is the B/W plate (480x300, 1:1) and the former the one
whose proportional painting was measured for SQ-0917.

Related: SQ-1037 (which exposes it), SQ-1036 (the face in question), SQ-1012 /
SQ-1024 (the same split, in the pixel lock), SQ-0790 (art_scale is the archive's),
SQ-1013 (machine knowledge belongs in zvm).
